Corrections these force on the first pass:
- Wood was too orange. It should be **pale maple** on the desks, warm oak on the floor.
- Dividers are **striped amber resin**, not plain glass.
- Pendants are **brass saucers**, not painted domes.
- Windows are **multi-pane grids**, not single sheets.
- Add: speaker on cinder blocks, framed art, chartreuse and checkerboard rugs.

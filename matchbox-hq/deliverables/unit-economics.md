## 🔁 WHAT CHANGED SINCE LAST VERSION

The old version of this file said a box costs **₹9**.

That number is dead. You changed the product.

| | Old version | This version |
|---|---|---|
| What a box is | one printed matchbox | a plain tray inside a printed outer box |
| Cost to make one | ₹9 | **₹12 to ₹20** |
| Plus freight in | ₹3 (was inside the ₹9) | ₹1.50 – ₹3 on top |
| Landed cost of one box | ₹9 | **₹13.50 to ₹23** |
| Prices I modelled | ₹49 / ₹79 / ₹99 | **₹79 and ₹99 only** |
| Break-even | ~150 boxes | **204 boxes** (see break-even.md) |

**Three things this changes:**

1. **₹49 is gone.** It was never modelled here again. A ₹49 box does not cover a ₹23 cost plus a ₹50 rider. Not in any version of the maths.
2. **₹79 now loses money the moment a rider is involved.** At the old ₹9 cost it scraped ₹8. At the new cost it goes negative.
3. **Drop 01 is smaller — 300 boxes, not 500.** The boxes cost twice as much, and the ₹20,000 did not grow. I would rather sell out 300 than sit on 500.

**What did NOT change:** GST 5%, no registration under ₹40 lakh, Razorpay 2.36%, direct UPI free, Borzo ₹40–45, couriers refuse matches, ₹20,000 total, Pune-only hand delivery.

## WHAT THIS MEANS

Five things to remember. Nothing else.

- **Sell at ₹99.** ₹79 does not survive a delivery charge.
- **A box now costs you about ₹18 to have in your hand.** It was ₹9.
- **Hand it over yourself and you keep ₹78.** Send it by rider and you keep ₹19.
- **You break even at about 204 boxes.** That is the only number to track.
- **Never let the bank balance drop below eight thousand rupees.** That is the stop line.

## Part 1 — What one box costs you to MAKE

Your box is now two things bought from two different places.

### Part A — the plain tray

**₹4 to ₹5 each.**

Bought once, in bulk, from Sivakasi.

**You buy these ONE TIME.** Drop 02 and Drop 03 use the leftovers from the same purchase. They do not pay for trays again.

### Part B — the printed outer box

**₹8 to ₹15 each.**

Short run, printed in Pune, new artwork every drop.

**You pay this EVERY drop.** New art means a new print run.

### Part C — freight on the trays

**₹1,500 for one road consignment, Sivakasi to Pune.**

Spread over however many trays you buy.

Buy 600 trays and that is **₹2.50 a tray**.

The printed outers are made in Pune, so they carry no freight.

### Put together — three honest cases

| | LOW | MID | HIGH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain tray | ₹4.00 | ₹4.50 | ₹5.00 |
| Printed outer | ₹8.00 | ₹11.00 | ₹15.00 |
| Freight share | ₹1.50 | ₹2.50 | ₹3.00 |
| **Landed cost, one box** | **₹13.50** | **₹18.00** | **₹23.00** |

**Plan on ₹18. Be ready for ₹23.**

Assumptions, said out loud:

- ₹4–5 assumes a wholesale lot of plain trays, not retail singles.
- ₹8–15 assumes a Pune short-run printer doing 300 rigid/folded outers with your artwork.
- ₹1.50 is what freight costs per tray if you buy 1,000 trays. ₹3 is what it costs if you only buy 500. Buying more trays makes each one cheaper.
- ⚠️ Supplier Scout confirmed most Sivakasi factories will not print under 50,000 boxes. That is exactly why this two-part model exists. Do not budget for a factory-printed short run — it does not exist at this size.

### 🚨 The shipping warning you must read

**Matches are UN 1944 flammable solids, Class 4.1.**

Couriers officially refuse them.

That means:

- Posting boxes across India is **not a plan**.
- **Drop 01 is Pune hand-delivery and Pune rider drops. Nothing else.**
- Anything outside Pune is a bonus, not a budget line.

## Part 3 — GST, in plain words

**The tax on matchboxes is 5%.**

Not 18%. A lot of websites still show the old number.

**You do not have to register for GST at all.**

The threshold for goods in Maharashtra is ₹40 lakh of turnover.

Drop 01 is under ₹30,000 of turnover.

So for the website, Instagram and cafés: **you charge no GST and file nothing.**

The one exception:

**The day you sell on Amazon or Flipkart, GST registration becomes compulsory — no matter how small you are.**

Sources:
- https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1606432 (all matches rationalised to one rate)
- https://www.gimbooks.com/blog/new-gst-rates-in-india-2025/ (22 Sep 2025 slab change, 12% → 5%)
- https://cleartax.in/s/gst-registration-limits-increased (₹40 lakh threshold for goods)
- https://tax2win.in/guide/gst-registration-amazon-seller (GST compulsory for marketplace sellers)

## Part 4 — What you actually keep, 2 prices × 4 channels × 3 cost levels

"What you keep" = money in, minus everything you paid out. Before your own time.

**Bold = makes money. 🔴 = loses money.**

### Channel 1 — Website order, delivered by rider, delivery free

You pay: landed box + ₹10 mailer + ₹50 rider + Razorpay fee.

#### At ₹79

| Cost level | What you keep |
|---|---|
| Low (₹13.50) | ₹3.60 |
| Mid (₹18.00) | 🔴 **−₹0.90 — YOU LOSE MONEY** |
| High (₹23.00) | 🔴 **−₹5.90 — YOU LOSE MONEY** |

#### At ₹99

| Cost level | What you keep |
|---|---|

| Mid (₹18.00) | **₹18.70** |

🔴 **READ THIS TWICE.** At ₹79, a rider delivery loses money at the mid and high cost. The ₹3.60 at the low cost is not a profit, it is a rounding error — one damaged box wipes out fifteen sales. **There is no version of ₹79 + free rider delivery that works.**

## Part 5 — ₹79 or ₹99? The plain answer

# 🔥 ₹99.

Not ₹79. Here is the whole argument in four lines.

**1. ₹79 breaks your delivery channel.**

At ₹79 a rider delivery keeps **−₹0.90** at the mid cost.

At ₹99 it keeps **₹18.70**.

That single ₹20 of price is the difference between a website that works and a website that is a charity.

**2. ₹79 does not break even inside Drop 01.**

At ₹79, mid cost, a normal sales mix, you need **303 boxes** to get your money back.

Drop 01 is **300 boxes**.

**You would sell out and still be short.**

At ₹99 you need 204. You get whole with 96 boxes to spare.

**3. ₹79 leaves you nothing to negotiate with.**

A café asking for a bigger cut, a supplier quoting the high end, one reprint — at ₹79 any one of those puts you underwater. At ₹99 you can absorb all three.

**4. ₹79 is not actually cheaper in the customer's head.**

₹79 and ₹99 are the same "under a hundred rupees" bracket to a Gen Z buyer in Pune. You give away ₹20 of margin and buy almost no extra demand. ₹99 is the cleaner number.

### The four rules that come with the price

1. **₹99 a box.** One price everywhere.
2. **3-box set at ₹297.** Push this one. It is the only thing that makes delivery cheap.
3. **Free delivery only on 2 boxes or more.** A single box gets handed over, picked up, or pays ₹50.
4. **Cafés at ₹50 a box, 25 at a time.** Never below ₹45.

### The one thing that would change my mind

If your printer quotes **₹8 an outer** and you sell almost everything by hand, ₹79 becomes survivable.

Even then it earns you less.

**Get the print quote first. Then price. Not the other way round.**

## 💡 THE THING TO UNDERSTAND ABOUT THIS CALENDAR

**Drop 01 is the tuition fee. Drop 02 is the business.**

You buy **600 trays** in Drop 01 and use **300**.
So Drop 02's trays are **already paid for** — a Drop 02 box costs **₹11, not ₹18**.

| You keep per box (handed over) | ₹78 | **₹85** |

🔒 **300 of those 600 trays are NOT YOURS TO TOUCH.**
Put them in a bag. Write **"DROP 02 — DO NOT OPEN"** on it. Every tray you steal from that bag costs Drop 02 **₹7** of margin.

## 📏 THE RULES THAT APPLY TO EVERY DROP

- **Pre-order window is never longer than 14 days.** Urgency dies on day 15.
- **Never open a window without announcing the close date on day 1.**
- **Never order stock before the pre-order gate is met.** Not once. Not "just a small run".
- **Never discount a drop.** Sold out is the marketing. A sale teaches everyone to wait.
- **Leftovers go to cafés, never to a clearance sale.**
- **Post the counter daily** — "X of 300 gone". Scarcity is the whole engine.
- **Announce Drop N+1 only after Drop N is 100% delivered.** Never both open at once.
- **Waitlist stays open forever**, between drops, always. It is the only thing that makes the next drop fast.
- **Max 20 free boxes per drop.** No exceptions, no "just one more".

### 🚪 What must be TRUE before Drop 02 starts

### The two things Drop 02 must test

1. **PRICE.** If Drop 01 sold out fast at ₹99, test **₹129** on 100 of the 300. You will never have more leverage to raise price than right after a sell-out. **Not testing it is the expensive mistake** — and a ₹11 box at ₹129 is a genuinely good business.
2. **A SECOND CITY — waitlist only, no promises.** Only if the outside-Pune waitlist is big, and only after a **surface-mode road courier confirms in email** that it will carry **UN 1944 Class 4.1** (`marketplace-roadmap.md`). **No email = no second city. Never improvise this one.**

### Why 300 and not more
The 300 free trays set the size. Going bigger means buying trays again and paying freight again — which throws away the entire reason the two-part model exists. **Sell 300 fast, then size Drop 03 properly.**

# The two-part production model

**Written 12 Sep 2026 - Production & Ops**

---

## The one-line version

You buy **plain match trays in bulk, once**. You print **new outer sleeves every drop**.
The expensive, changing part (the artwork) stays small. The cheap, boring part (the matches) gets bought big and cheap.

---

## RECOMMENDED FIRST PURCHASE

> ### 1,000 plain trays + 500 printed outers
> ### Total: **₹12,500**
> (₹1,500 samples + ₹5,500 trays landed + ₹5,500 printed outers)

That leaves **₹7,500** of your ₹20,000 for mailers, domain, legal and ad float.
It does not touch the ceiling.

## Why 1,000 trays and not 500 or 2,000

Freight from Sivakasi is roughly the same whether you ship 500 boxes or 2,000. That is the whole argument.

| You buy | Price/tray | Goods | Freight | **Landed cost per tray** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 500 | ~₹5.00 | ₹2,500 | ₹1,000 | **₹7.00** |
| **1,000** | **~₹4.50** | **₹4,500** | **₹1,000** | **₹5.50** |
| 2,000 | ~₹4.20 | ₹8,400 | ₹1,200 | ₹4.80 |
| 5,000 | ~₹4.00 | ₹20,000 | ₹1,500 | ₹4.30 |

- 500 to 1,000 cuts your landed cost by **21%** for ₹3,000 more cash. Worth it.
- 1,000 to 2,000 cuts it by only **13%** for another ₹3,900. Not worth it yet.
- 5,000 spends **your entire business budget** on matches. Never.

**1,000 trays is the knee in the curve.** It covers Drop 01 and Drop 02.

## What a box actually costs you

### Drop 01 (charging only the 500 trays you actually use)

| | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Tray | 4.50 |
| Share of freight | 1.00 |
| Printed outer | 11.00 |
| **Cost per box** | **₹16.50** |

Sell at ₹99 → **₹82.50 gross margin per box.**
Sell at ₹79 → ₹62.50.

### Drop 02 - the trays are already paid for

| | ₹ |
|---|---|
| Tray | **0 cash** (sitting in your room) |
| Printed outer | 11.00 (maybe 9 - the die already exists) |
| **Cash out per box** | **₹11.00** |

**Drop 02 costs you 33% less cash per box than Drop 01.** That is the whole point of the model.

### Drop 03 - you run out of trays

1,000 trays = Drop 01 + Drop 02. **Re-buy trays during Drop 02, not after.**
By then you will have real money from Drop 01, so buy 2,000 and get the ₹4.80 landed price.

## The printed outer is your real cost, not the matches

Look at the ₹16.50 again. The matches are ₹5.50 of it. **The printed card is ₹11.**

So the three things that move your cost are all at the printer:

1. **Die charge** - one-time ₹1,500-3,000 for the cutting die. Ask if it is reusable for Drop 02. It is - same box size. Make them say it out loud.
2. **Digital, not offset** - at 500 pieces, offset means paying for printing plates. Digital has no plate charge. Offset only wins above about 2,000 pieces.
3. **Finishes** - foil, spot UV and embossing each add a machine pass and real money. **Matte lamination only on Drop 01.** Add the shiny stuff on Drop 02 when you know it sells.

### Outer price falls with quantity too

| Outers | Est. ₹/outer |
|---|---|
| 250 | ~15 |
| **500** | **~11** |
| 1,000 | ~8 |

Tempting to print 1,000 outers at ₹8. **Do not.** New artwork every drop is the product. Printing 1,000 of one design locks you into it.

## The alternative: just buy finished custom-printed boxes from Sivakasi

Worth taking seriously for 30 seconds, then rejecting.

**What you would get:** one vendor, one invoice, finished printed matchboxes delivered.

**Why it does not work for you:**

- **The minimum kills it.** Sivakasi custom print runs start in the thousands. At an MOQ of 5,000 boxes you spend roughly **₹25,000** - more than your entire ₹20,000 budget, before a single sale.
- **You get one design, 5,000 times.** A drop brand with one design is not a drop brand.
- **The print is flexo on cheap board.** It is built to survive a kirana shelf, not to look good on Instagram. Your whole product is how it looks.
- **Lead time doubles.** 3-4 weeks from Sivakasi for custom print vs 7-10 working days at a Pune printer for outers. You cannot react to anything.
- **Artwork changes cost a new plate.** Every time.

**Verdict: the two-part model is right.** You pay about ₹3-5 more per box, and you buy the ability to change the design every drop, start at 500 instead of 5,000, and keep ₹12,500 of spend instead of ₹25,000.

Revisit finished boxes only if you are ever selling 5,000+ of one design. Not this year.

---

## What the trays unlock later

Once 1,000 trays are in your room, your cost to launch a new drop is **just the printing**.

- Test a design on 100 boxes: ₹1,500 of printing. That is it.
- Collab with a cafe on their own sleeve: 200 boxes, ~₹3,000.
- Kill a design that flops: you lost the printing, not the matches.

**Trays are the asset. Sleeves are the experiment.**

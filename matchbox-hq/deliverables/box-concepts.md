# 6 Box Concepts — all buildable by you, in Canva, alone

**Rules I designed inside:**
- Max **3 colours + black**. Cheap printing, loud shelf.
- Every concept works in **flat colour + type only**. No illustration skill needed. No photos.
- Font pairs are all **free in Canva**.
- Every box has the same back panel (the legal block) so you only build it once and copy-paste it.
- Every box has **one reason to be photographed**. If I couldn't name it, I cut the concept.

**Note:** `[BRAND]` = whatever wins decision D-002.

---

## THE ONE THING TO GET RIGHT
The box gets seen at **3cm on a café counter** and at **1000px on a phone screen**. Nothing in between.
So: **one giant element per face.** No paragraphs on the front. Ever.

**Canva setup (do this once):**
- New custom size: **50 × 35 mm** for the front/back panel + **50 × 15 mm** strikers. Ask Supplier agent for the real die-line before printing — this is just for designing.
- Make the front panel a **Canva Brand Template**, then duplicate 6 times. That's your whole Drop 01.

# 1. काडेपेटी CLASSIC (the hero)
**Idea:** The Marathi word for "matchbox", set huge, like a government sign or an old Deccan cinema ticket.

| Face | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Front** | The word **काडेपेटी** in Devanagari, filling 80% of the panel, cream on deep black. Tiny "No. 01" in the top-right corner. |
| **Back** | Legal block (below). |
| **Side 1** | Striker strip. Left raw/brown. |
| **Side 2** | Striker strip + tiny repeated "[BRAND] • PUNE • [BRAND] • PUNE" |
| **Top/bottom** | Solid pink. So a stack of boxes reads as a pink bar. |

**Palette:** Ink Black `#0B0B0B` · Bone `#F2EAD9` · Hot Pink `#FF2D78`
**Type:** Devanagari — Canva's *Baloo 2* or *Mukta* (Bold). Latin — *Archivo Black* for numbers.
**The reference:** Old Marathi signboards + Sivakasi factory labels. It looks like it's been made for 60 years. It hasn't.
**Why photographed:** It's the most "screenshot-worthy Indian design" thing on the list — Devanagari-as-graphic is huge with Indian design accounts. This is the one that gets reposted by design pages.

## 2. PINKHEAD
**Idea:** The product, drawn as a diagram. One match, dead centre, enormous.

| Face | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Front** | One single match, vertical, black stick + pink head, drawn from **two Canva rectangles + one rounded rectangle**. That's it. Nothing else. |
| **Back** | Legal block. Above it, tiny: "50 sticks. 50 small fires." |
| **Sides** | Striker. On the other side, "PINKHEAD" in 6pt letterspaced caps. |

**Palette:** Off-White `#FAF7F2` · Ink Black `#0B0B0B` · Hot Pink `#FF2D78`
**Type:** *Archivo* / *Inter* — tiny, wide letterspacing, all caps. Swiss, clean, expensive-looking.
**The reference:** Muji / Aesop minimalism, but for ₹79. The joke is the restraint.
**Why photographed:** It's the flat-lay box. Sits on a marble café table next to a cortado and looks like it cost ₹400. This is the one cafés will actually want on their counter.

## 3. LOADSHEDDING
**Idea:** A love letter to the power cut. The oldest shared Indian memory there is.

| Face | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Front** | Solid black. One small pink dot, off-centre, like a distant flame. Small white text bottom-left: **"लाईट गेली."** (The lights went.) |
| **Back** | Legal block + one line: "For when MSEB decides." |
| **Sides** | Striker. Other side: a tiny row of white dashes, like a fuse. |

**Palette:** True Black `#000000` · Warm White `#FFFFFF` · Ember Pink `#FF3D6E`
**Type:** *Mukta* Light for Devanagari, *Inter* Light for Latin. Small, quiet, a lot of empty black.
**The reference:** Every Indian childhood. Marathi "लाईट गेली" is the exact phrase — not "power cut". That specificity is the whole joke.
**Why photographed:** Because it's 95% black, it *disappears* in a photo except the flame. Best box to shoot in an actually dark room with a lit match. Made for Reels.

## 4. FC ROAD / KP / HINJEWADI — the Pune Series
**Idea:** Three boxes, one per neighbourhood. Instant collectible set. This is your collecting engine.

| Face | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Front** | The area name in huge condensed caps — **FC ROAD** / **KOREGAON PARK** / **HINJEWADI** — each on its own colour. Below it, tiny: the pin code (411004 / 411001 / 411057). |
| **Back** | Legal block + "3 of 3. Collect the city." |
| **Sides** | Striker. Other side: a fake "route number" like a PMPML bus board. |

**Palette (one per box):** FC Road = Pink `#FF2D78` on Black · KP = Deep Green `#0E3B2E` on Bone `#F2EAD9` · Hinjewadi = Steel Blue `#1B3A5C` on Bone
**Type:** *Anton* or *Archivo Black*, squeezed tall. Bus-board energy.
**The reference:** PMPML destination boards + area rivalry. Hinjewadi people joke about the traffic, KP people about the cafés, FC Road about the crowd.
**Why photographed:** **Tribalism.** People post the box for *their* area. "obviously Hinjewadi 💀" is a free caption from someone else's account. It also forces a 3-pack purchase.

## 5. THE STRIKE INSTRUCTION
**Idea:** A deadpan instruction manual for something everyone already knows how to do.

| Face | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Front** | Three numbered steps in tiny type with three simple icons (rectangle, line, dot): **1. HOLD** / **2. STRIKE** / **3. DON'T PANIC**. |
| **Back** | Legal block + a fake "Fig. 4" diagram of a hand. (Canva has a free line-icon hand — or just use an arrow.) |
| **Sides** | Striker. Other side: "MADE FOR HUMANS WITH THUMBS". |

**Palette:** Bone `#F2EAD9` · Ink `#0B0B0B` · Safety Orange `#FF5A1F`
**Type:** *Space Mono* or *IBM Plex Mono*. Technical-manual monospace does all the comedy for free.
**The reference:** IKEA instructions + safety placards. Gen Z humour = extremely serious tone about an extremely stupid thing.
**Why photographed:** It reads like a meme but is genuinely well-designed. People photograph things that make them look funny *and* tasteful. This does both.

## 6. BURN BOOK
**Idea:** The box as a tiny diary you're not supposed to read.

| Face | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Front** | Hot pink, and looks like a scribbled-on notebook cover: **"BURN BOOK"** in handwritten script, one doodle heart, one crossed-out scribble. |
| **Back** | Legal block + tiny handwritten: "don't read this. light something." |
| **Sides** | Striker. Other side: a row of handwritten tally marks. |

**Palette:** Hot Pink `#FF2D78` · Black `#0B0B0B` · Paper White `#FFFFFF`
**Type:** Canva handwriting fonts — *Caveat* or *Shadows Into Light*. Deliberately messy.
**The reference:** Mean Girls. Still fully alive on Gen Z Instagram. Also the "keep a tiny secret object" instinct.
**Why photographed:** It's the most **giftable** box on the list — it's what you hand a friend. Girls' group chats will move this one.
**⚠️ Flag:** Use "Burn Book" as a **box name only**, never as the company name. It's a film reference and legally soft. Told you the same in brand-names.md.

# THE BACK PANEL (build once, paste on all 6)

⚠️ **Legal agent is writing the exact required wording (task T7). These are placeholders — swap in their text before printing.**

Set in 5–6pt, *Inter* or *Arial*, left-aligned, high contrast, boring on purpose. Boring = compliant.

```
[BRAND NAME] — SAFETY MATCHES
Marketed by: [LEGAL ENTITY NAME]
[FULL ADDRESS WITH PIN CODE], Pune, Maharashtra
Net Quantity: [XX] Matchsticks (N)
MRP ₹[XX.00] (incl. of all taxes)
Packed: [MM/YYYY]        Batch: [01]
Country of origin: India
Customer Care: [NAME], [PHONE], [EMAIL]
Safety: Keep away from children. Close box before striking.
Store in a dry place.
```

**⚠️ Also required on the FRONT:** the words **"Safety Matches"** must appear somewhere legible on the pack — the generic name, not just your brand name. Set it small under the logo on every one of the 6 designs. Legal covers this in packaging-label-rules.md.

**Non-negotiables when you lay it out:**
- Legal text gets its **own zone**. Never overlapping artwork. Never pink-on-pink.
- Minimum **6pt**, and **letter width at least one-third of letter height** (a real rule — don't use a condensed font here). If it's unreadable it's non-compliant.
- Never grey-on-grey. Black on a light panel. Legal text must be legible.
- Leave a 2mm safe margin from every edge. Match printing is not precise.
- Keep an editable master in Canva. This block **will** change once Legal (T7) lands.

# 🎯 DROP 01 RECOMMENDATION — print these 3

### 1. काडेपेटी CLASSIC — the identity box
This is the brand, not just a design. It teaches people your name in one glance and it's the one design accounts repost. If you print one thing, print this.

### 2. PINKHEAD — the counter box
The only concept here that a café owner will say yes to without thinking. Minimal, tasteful, doesn't look like merch. This is the box that gets you distribution.

### 3. LOADSHEDDING — the Reel box
Built for the dark-room strike video, which is your single best-performing content format. Also the most emotionally Indian of the six — it does the sharing for you.

**Why these three together:** one is *identity*, one is *retail-friendly*, one is *content-friendly*. They don't overlap. And they only need 4 ink colours total across the whole run — pink, black, bone, white — which keeps the print quote low. (Check that against Supplier's quote in T3.)

**Split of 500:** 200 Kaadepeti Classic / 200 Pinkhead / 100 Loadshedding.
Loadshedding gets the short run **on purpose** — "only 100 made" is a real scarcity line for Week 4.

**Hold back for Drop 02:** the Pune Series (FC Road / KP / Hinjewadi). It's your best collecting idea, but a 3-box set only works once people already want one box. Launch it as "Drop 02: the city set" and it'll sell itself.

**Park:** Strike Instruction (good, but it's a joke box — better as a free insert or sticker), Burn Book (great gift SKU for Valentine's / Diwali).

# 🔴 STOP — MATCHES ARE DANGEROUS GOODS

This is not a technicality. **It is the single biggest constraint on this business.**

### The facts

- Safety matches have their own UN dangerous-goods number: **UN 1944**, **Class 4.1 (flammable solids)**, **Packing Group III**.
  ([hazmattool.com/UN1944](https://www.hazmattool.com/info.php?b=UN1944), [adrdangerousgoods.com — UN 1944](https://adrdangerousgoods.com/eng/substances/0001279/un1944-matches-safety-book-card-or-strike-on-box/))
- **Shiprocket** — the aggregator almost every small Indian D2C brand uses — lists **matchboxes and matches on its prohibited/dangerous list.**
  ([Shiprocket support: prohibited/dangerous items](https://support.shiprocket.in/support/solutions/articles/43000460506-which-products-are-prohibited-dangerous-to-ship-via-shiprocket-), [Shiprocket prohibited items](https://www.shiprocket.in/x/prohibited-items/))
- **Blue Dart** bans **all IATA-restricted and dangerous goods on all of its services.**
  ([Blue Dart banned commodities](https://www.bluedart.com/banned-commodities))
- **India Post** prohibits any **explosive, inflammable, dangerous or deleterious substance** in the post.
  ([India Post — 9 classes of dangerous goods, PDF](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/prohibited_items/Definitions_of_9_Classes_of_Dangerous_Goods.pdf), [Prohibited Articles](https://odishapost.gov.in/EN/ProhibitedArticles.aspx))
- **Ekart** (Flipkart's own logistics arm) runs a formal dangerous/restricted goods policy.
  ([Ekart dangerous & restricted goods policy](https://www.ekartlogistics.in/dangerous-and-restricted-goods-policy))
- **Amazon India** requires sellers to declare dangerous goods and submit an **MSDS / exemption sheet**; DG items face extra restrictions on FBA and on Amazon's own delivery network.
  ([Amazon India: what are dangerous goods](https://sell.amazon.in/seller-blog/what-are-dangerous-goods), [Amazon Shipping India: dangerous goods](https://shipping.amazon.in/blog/dangerous-goods-indian-ecommerce-shipping))

### What this means in plain English

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can you courier a matchbox to Delhi from your house? | **Almost certainly no.** Standard aggregators prohibit it. |
| Can you air-ship matches? | **No.** Air is the strictest mode. |
| Can you put them in India Post? | **No.** Inflammable substances are prohibited. |
| Can you deliver in Pune by hand or bike? | **YES.** No courier network involved. |
| Can matches move Sivakasi → Pune in bulk? | **YES** — by licensed road/goods transport. That's how every matchbox in India travels. |
| Can you list on Amazon/Flipkart today? | **No.** Not until a compliant shipping route exists. |

### The consequence — say it out loud

> **Your "Pune only at launch" decision was not a modest start. It was the only legal, workable option.**
> Hand-delivery inside Pune completely sidesteps the dangerous-goods problem.
> **Everything on the channel list after Instagram is blocked until you solve shipping.**

### The workarounds Indian sellers actually use

1. **Surface / road only, never air.** DG rules relax on ground. Declare correctly; **never mis-declare** — mis-declared DG gets seized and can void insurance.
2. **Ask couriers in writing, before you promise anyone anything.** Contact business/enterprise desks at surface-network couriers (Delhivery Surface, DTDC, Professional Couriers, Trackon, regional road transporters) and ask one exact question: *"Do you accept UN 1944, Class 4.1 PG III safety matches, packing group III, surface mode, B2C, under 25 kg?"* **Get the yes in email.** Verbal yes from a pickup boy is worth nothing.
   - **I could not verify which of these will say yes.** Do not assume. Ask.
3. **Licensed goods-transport (LTL road freight) for bulk only.** Perfect for factory→you. Useless for one box to one customer.
4. **Local delivery apps for intra-city.** Porter / Borzo / Dunzo-style same-city two-wheeler delivery is a road move within Pune, not a courier network. Still ask, still declare.
5. **The collectible workaround: sell the object, not the fire.** For orders outside Pune, ship the **printed collectible box empty or with the striker only**, and say so honestly on the product page. It is legal, cheap and shippable — and for a *collectible* brand, buyers outside Pune are buying the artwork anyway. Pune buyers get the filled box, hand-delivered.
6. **Pickup points.** Let Pune customers collect from a stocked café. Zero delivery cost, zero DG risk, and it drives café footfall — which is also your pitch to the café.

**⚠️ Limitation on this research:** direct page fetches were blocked in this environment. Every fact above comes from search-result summaries of those official pages. **Open each link yourself and confirm before you act on it.** Policies change.

# 1. AMAZON INDIA

### Entry requirements
- [ ] **GSTIN** (regular registration — the composition scheme is barred for e-commerce sellers)
- [ ] **PAN**
- [ ] **Active bank account** + cancelled cheque
- [ ] **Pickup address in the same state as the GST registration**
- [ ] Email + mobile
- [ ] **Dangerous-goods declaration / MSDS or exemption sheet** for the matches ← **the hard one**
- [ ] Category listing approval (matches sit in a lighters/matches-type sub-category — **verify the exact node and whether it is gated**; I could not confirm this)

### Real costs
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Account creation | **₹0** — no signup fee, no monthly subscription on the basic account |
| Referral fee | **2%–38%** depending on category. As of March 2026 Amazon India extended **zero referral fee** to a large set of products under **₹1,000** across 1,800+ categories — **you must check whether matches qualify** |
| Closing fee | roughly **₹20–₹26** in low price bands |
| Weight handling | from **₹29**, rising with weight and distance |
| GST on all fees | **+18%** on top |

Source: [Amazon India seller guide (Unicommerce)](https://unicommerce.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-amazon-india/), official portal: [sellercentral.amazon.in](https://sellercentral.amazon.in)

### The maths at ₹99
Fees of roughly ₹25–₹50 per order against a ₹99 product with ₹16 cost. **A single box on Amazon is not worth shipping.** If you ever go there, go with a **3-box or 5-box set at ₹249–₹399** — that is the only version that survives the fee structure.

### Realistic timeline
- Account live: **2–7 days** once GST exists.
- Dangerous-goods clearance + a courier that will actually carry them: **unknown, likely months, possibly never for a one-person brand.** Be honest with yourself about this.

### 🚦 Trigger to apply
**All four, or don't bother:**
1. GST registered and filing
2. A courier has confirmed **in writing** it will carry UN 1944 surface
3. You are producing **1,000+ boxes per drop**
4. You have a **multi-box set priced ₹249+**

**Earliest realistic:** after Drop 03. **Not in 2026.**

# 2. FLIPKART

### Entry requirements
- [ ] **GSTIN** — mandatory from the first rupee, **regular registration, not composition** (CGST Act s.10(2)(d))
- [ ] **PAN**
- [ ] Bank account + business address
- [ ] Register at **[seller.flipkart.com](https://seller.flipkart.com)**
- [ ] Compliance with Flipkart's prohibited/restricted list, and **Ekart's dangerous goods policy** for anything Ekart carries

### Real costs
- Registration: **₹0** — no upfront fee, deposit or subscription.
- Commission: varies by category. **I could not verify the rate for a matches/lighters category — check the live rate card in Seller Hub.** Expect commission + collection fee + shipping fee + 18% GST on all of it.

Sources: [Flipkart seller registration guide](https://swcybernetics.in/guides/flipkart-seller-registration), [Flipkart GST requirement](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/flipkart-gst-registration-sellers-guide), [Ekart DG policy](https://www.ekartlogistics.in/dangerous-and-restricted-goods-policy)

### Realistic timeline
- Account verification: **24–72 hours** with a valid GST.
- **Same dangerous-goods wall as Amazon.** Ekart has an explicit DG policy; Class 4.1 is very unlikely to pass in standard B2C parcels.

### 🚦 Trigger to apply
Same four conditions as Amazon. **Do Amazon first** — one marketplace at a time. Running two while shipping 500 boxes by hand will break you.

**Earliest realistic:** after Amazon is working. **2027.**

# 3. BLINKIT (quick commerce)

### How it actually works — understand this before you dream about it
Blinkit is **not a marketplace.** You do not "list" and wait for sales.
Blinkit **raises a Purchase Order**, buys your stock at an agreed **trade price**, holds it in its own dark stores under its own GSTIN, and retails it. A **Category Manager decides yes or no** — on demand for the category, brand recognition, price competitiveness, packaging that survives dark-store handling, and **whether you can keep stock consistent across many stores.**

### Entry requirements
- [ ] **GSTIN**
- [ ] **FSSAI licence** (food/grocery only — likely not applicable to you, **confirm**)
- [ ] **Trademark certificate or brand authorisation letter** ← you will need the trademark
- [ ] Bank account + KYC
- [ ] **Barcode / GTIN** on every SKU
- [ ] Product catalogue with compliant images
- [ ] Register at the official partner portal: **[partner.blinkit.com](https://partner.blinkit.com)**
- [ ] **Dangerous-goods acceptance for dark-store storage and two-wheeler delivery — I could not verify Blinkit's policy on matches. Ask them directly. Do not assume they will take a flammable solid.**

### Real costs
- Onboarding itself: **free through the official portal.**
- **⚠️ Do not pay an onboarding agency.** Search results are dominated by agencies charging fees to do a free form. Go direct.
- Trade margin: quick commerce typically demands a **deep margin plus listing and marketing contributions**. **I could not verify Blinkit's specific numbers** — treat any figure you see on an agency blog as unverified.

Sources: [How to sell on Blinkit (WareIQ)](https://wareiq.com/resources/blogs/how-to-sell-on-blinkit/), [Blinkit onboarding guide (Digiskala)](https://digiskala.com/blinkit-seller-onboarding-guide/)

### Realistic timeline
**1–4 weeks** for documents and account activation *if* a Category Manager says yes. Getting the yes is the hard part, not the paperwork.

### 🚦 Trigger to apply
- [ ] Registered **trademark**
- [ ] **Barcodes/GTIN** on every SKU
- [ ] Can produce and hold **3,000+ units/month**
- [ ] Can survive **60–90 day payment terms** without going broke
- [ ] Can fund a deep trade margin at your price point
- [ ] Written confirmation they will handle a Class 4.1 product

**Earliest realistic:** Drop 05+. **You are 12–18 months away. Do not spend an hour on this in 2026.**

| # | Channel | Blocked by | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Own pre-order site** | Nothing. Hand-delivery in Pune. | **NOW** |
| 2 | **Instagram** | Nothing. | **NOW** |

| 4 | Rest of India (D2C) | **Dangerous goods.** Needs a courier's written yes, or the empty-box version. | Drop 02–03 |
| 5 | Amazon | GST + DG clearance + set pricing + volume | Drop 03+ |
| 6 | Flipkart | Same, after Amazon works | 2027 |
| 7 | Blinkit | Trademark + barcodes + 3,000 units/mo + credit terms | Drop 05+ |
| 8 | Zepto | Everything Blinkit needs, and harder | After Blinkit |

### The one-line takeaway

> **Channels 1–3 need nothing but your legs. Channels 4–8 need a dangerous-goods solution you do not have yet.**
> **Sell 500 boxes in Pune first. Solve shipping second. Marketplaces last.**

### Free things to do now that cost ₹0 and unlock everything later
- [ ] Get the **brand authorisation / NOC letter** from your Sivakasi supplier. One email. Free. Needed by every platform above.
- [ ] Get the **MSDS / product safety sheet** from the supplier. Free. Amazon will ask for it.
- [ ] Email **3 surface couriers** the exact UN 1944 question. Free. The answer decides whether channel 4 exists at all.
- [ ] Ask the supplier **how they ship matches across India**. They do it every week. They already know the answer. **Free, and it is the fastest route to solving this.**

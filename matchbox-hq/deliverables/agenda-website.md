## 4. WHAT THE PAGE MUST PROVE IN 5 SECONDS

A stranger lands from a Reel, on a phone, with 5 seconds of patience. Above the fold, no scrolling, in this order:

1. **It is real.** One photo of the actual object. The single most common reason a Gen Z buyer bounces off a new Indian brand is thinking it's dropshipped.
2. **What it costs. ₹99.** Big. No "starting from".
3. **When they get it, and where.** *"Hand-delivered in Pune, 27 Oct – 2 Nov."* **Say Pune-only out loud, above the fold.** Matches are UN 1944 — we cannot courier them, and finding that out at checkout is a refund.
4. **How few there are. "X of 300."**
5. **One button.** Not two. Not a menu.

**What must NOT be up there:** a logo carousel, an "our story" paragraph, a newsletter popup, a hero video, stock photos, or the word "premium".

**Look and feel:** black page, the box is the only colour, one typeface, huge type, enormous whitespace. **It should look like the box** — if the page and the box don't look related, the page is wrong. And it should load in **under 2 seconds on 4G in Hinjewadi**, which means one image, no fonts from a CDN, no libraries.

## 5. THE FOUR STATES (already built — `website-guide.md`, Part 3D)

| State | Visitor sees | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Teaser** | Sketch + "something is coming" + email field | now → 21 Sep |
| **Waitlist** | "Pre-orders open 29 Sep" + name/phone. **No money.** | 22 – 28 Sep |
| **Open** | ₹99 · live counter · pay button · close date **stated on day one** | **29 Sep – 5 Oct** |
| **Closed / Sold out** | "300/300 gone. Drop 02 waitlist →" | from 5 Oct |

**Two rules that are easy to get wrong:**
- **Never open a window without the close date visible from minute one.** Urgency you announce on the last day isn't urgency.
- **The waitlist never closes.** It stays open forever, between drops, always. It is the only thing that makes Drop 02 fast.

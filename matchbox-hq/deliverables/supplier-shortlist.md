# REALITY CHECK

## Is ₹8,000 for 500 custom-printed boxes realistic?

**Short answer: the ₹8,000 is probably fine. The 500 is the problem.**

### The money side looks OK

- A plain matchbox in India sells at roughly **₹1–2 each**. One Sivakasi listing prices a full safety matchbox at **₹1.00**.
- Custom artwork, coloured heads and black sticks are add-ons on top of that.
- ₹8,000 ÷ 500 = **₹16 per box** of budget. That is a lot of headroom over a ₹1 base box.
- At a ₹49–99 retail price, ₹16 landed cost is a healthy margin.

**So your budget per box is not the blocker.**

### The quantity is the blocker

This is the honest bad news:

- Geewin's own published guidance: most Sivakasi factories private-label from **50,000 to 1,00,000 boxes**.
- Even their lower "custom matchstick box" tier starts at **5,000 units**.
- A coloured match head is a **chemical change to the head paste**. The factory has to mix a batch and run the machine. They will not do that for 500 boxes.
- Black-dyed sticks means dyeing the splints. Same problem — that's a separate process run.

**Realistic expectation:** if you insist on factory-made pink/black heads on black sticks in a printed box, the real first order is **5,000 boxes minimum**, quite possibly 50,000.

### Likely true price per box

I did **not** find a published price for a custom matchbox with coloured heads. Anyone quoting you one from memory is guessing. Here is my honest read, clearly labelled as an estimate:

| What you order | Realistic ₹/box | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Plain stock matchbox, no customisation | **₹1 – ₹2** | Good — seen listed |
| Stock box + your printed label/sleeve (you do the printing) | **₹3 – ₹6** | Estimate |
| Factory custom printed box, plain heads, 5,000 units | **₹4 – ₹10** | Estimate |
| Factory custom box + coloured heads + black sticks, 5,000 units | **₹8 – ₹18** | Estimate — needs quotes |
| Same, at only 500 units (if anyone agrees) | **₹25 – ₹60** | Estimate — small-run penalty is brutal |

**Treat every number above as a guess until three real quotes come back.** That is exactly what the quote tracker is for.

## The cheapest honest workaround for a 500-box test

**Buy plain. Print your own wrap.**

1. Buy **500 plain stock matchboxes** from a Sivakasi maker. Ask specifically if they already stock **black heads** — Sundaravel already makes a black-head match, so this may cost you nothing extra. Budget **₹1–2 each = ₹500–₹1,000**.
2. Print your meme/art design as a **wrap-around sticker or paper sleeve** in Pune. Pune digital printers do genuinely tiny runs — one lists a minimum order of **20 stickers**, another **25 pieces**, with 1-day local delivery. Budget roughly **₹3–₹6 each = ₹1,500–₹3,000**.
3. Stick them on yourself. 500 boxes is one evening with a friend and a playlist.

**All-in: roughly ₹2,000 – ₹4,000. Well inside your ₹8,000.**

Sources for the Pune printing option: https://printposters.in/bulk-stickers · https://picatypeprinting.com/corporate-stationery/labels-stickers/ · https://printo.in/categories/stickers-and-labels

### Why this is actually the smart move, not the cheap move

- You find out if Gen Z Pune will pay ₹49–99 **before** you commit to 5,000 units.
- A hand-applied sleeve looks *more* collectible and indie, not less.
- **Pink heads are the one thing you cannot fake with a sticker.** If pink is non-negotiable for the brand, you must wait until you can order 5,000. Decide that now, not after you've spent money.

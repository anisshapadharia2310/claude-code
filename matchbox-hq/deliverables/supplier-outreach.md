# Enquiry Messages — Copy, Paste, Send

**How to use:** Fill in the `[square brackets]`. Nothing else needs changing.

**Send order:** WhatsApp first. It gets answered fastest in Sivakasi. Email only if they ask for it or if you want a paper trail.

**Send to all 10 at once.** Don't wait for replies one by one. Expect 3–5 to answer.

## Small things that will save you money

- **Never accept "price on call" only.** Ask for it in writing on WhatsApp. You need it in the tracker.
- **Ask GST every single time.** A ₹10 quote that becomes ₹11.80 after GST ruins your margin maths.
- **Get the GSTIN.** No GSTIN means no input credit and probably no invoice. Walk away.
- **Never pay 100% advance** to a supplier you found on a directory. 30–50% advance, balance before dispatch, is normal.
- **Order a sample before the bulk order.** Always. Even if they charge for it.
- **If they say "minimum 50,000"** — don't argue. Ask this instead: *"Can you sell me 500 plain boxes with black heads, and I will print my own sleeve?"* That question often gets a yes.

## What "no" sounds like, and what to say back

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Minimum 50,000 boxes" | "Understood. Can I buy 500 plain boxes now and place the custom order later once I have sold through?" |
| "Pink is not possible" | "What head colours can you do today without a special batch?" |
| "We don't print boxes" | "No problem — can you supply plain boxes and recommend a label printer in Sivakasi?" |
| "Send design first" | "Happy to — could you first confirm your MOQ and rough price so I know we're a fit?" |

## ⚠️ READ THIS BEFORE YOU POST ANYTHING — the tobacco rule

Indian law (COTPA + the ad rules around it) is **strict** about anything that promotes or even hints at tobacco. Surrogate advertising is taken seriously. A meme account can get away with it; a registered brand selling a physical product cannot.

**Hard bans for this account — no exceptions:**
- ❌ Never show a cigarette, bidi, joint, rolling paper, filter tips, an ashtray, or a pack. Not even out of focus in a corner.
- ❌ Never write "for smokers", "smoke break", "sutta", "chill session", or any wink at it.
- ❌ Never repost UGC that has any of the above in frame. Even if it's a great photo. **Especially** if it's a great photo.
- ❌ No "light up" captions where "up" is doing suspicious work.

**What you light on camera instead** — all safe, all beautiful:
candles · diyas · incense · birthday cake · a gas stove · a campfire · a sparkler · a fireplace · a lantern · nothing at all (just the match burning in the dark).

**You will lose a slice of the IT-park audience by doing this. Take the loss.** One screenshot of a tobacco-adjacent post is enough to cost you marketplace listings, café partnerships and, at worst, a legal notice. The brand is **fire, art, and collecting.** Hold that line in every single caption.

If someone comments about smoking: don't reply, don't delete, don't engage. Just leave it.

## 5 REEL HOOKS THAT WORK FOR THIS PRODUCT
Use these on repeat. They're your bread and butter.

1. **The dark room strike.** Lights fully off. Phone propped. Strike. The flare blows out the exposure for half a second, then the face of the box appears in the glow. **No music for the first 2 seconds — just the sound.** Best hook you own.
2. **ASMR close-up.** Phone 10cm from the box. The scrape, the catch, the tiny crackle. Caption on screen: "turn your sound on". People finish this one.
3. **Hands-only unboxing.** No face, no talking. Courier bag → scissors → box in palm → thumb pushes the drawer open → one match out. 11 seconds.
4. **The collection wall.** Boxes laid out in a grid on a black table, one gets picked up, gap in the grid. Says "collectible" without using the word.
5. **Pack-an-order POV.** Your desk, an order notification, you writing the name by hand on the bag. People love watching a tiny business work.

**Phone rules:** shoot 9:16 · lock exposure before you strike or the flare wrecks it · clean your lens · film near a window in the day and in full dark at night · shoot 4 takes, keep 1.

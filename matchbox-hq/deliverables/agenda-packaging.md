## 3. DECISIONS YOU MUST MAKE

### ⚠️ A. Who puts the STRIKER on the box? — **answer this first, it changes everything**
Nobody on this team has answered it yet. The striker strip normally lives on the **outer**, and the outer is now the thing *we* print. A Pune digital printer **cannot** apply striker paste.

| | Option | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Full printed sleeve, Sivakasi applies the striker to our card** | Best-looking. Depends entirely on a vendor saying yes. This is what `production-model.md` assumes — but nobody has confirmed it. |
| B | **Printed belly band over a factory plain box that already has strikers** | Band wraps top → end → bottom → end (**~108 × 46 mm**), leaving both striker sides untouched. Cheapest, zero striker risk, **no die needed** — it is a straight rectangle. Slightly less "finished". |
| C | **Full printed sleeve + self-adhesive striker tape applied by hand** | **600 strips** (2 per box). Two evenings. One crooked strip per box is very likely. |

**→ My recommendation: ask for A on Monday's calls, lock B as the fallback by Fri 18 Sep.**
If no vendor will strike our card, we go band and we do not lose a day. Revisit A for Drop 02.

### B. The quality spec may change the box size
You want export/hotel grade: **wooden veneer tray, poplar/aspen splints, black head, carbonised stick, one-strike, damp-proof.** Hotel/export matches are often **longer** than the ₹2 kirana box. If the tray is 55 or 60 mm, every number below is wrong.
**→ Recommendation: no die, no final artwork until a real tray is in your hand and measured. Design on the placeholder, commit nothing.**

### C. Print method
| | Option | At 300 units |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Digital (HP Indigo-type)** | No plate charge. ~₹13–15 per sleeve. |
| B | Offset | Plates cost more than the whole run. Only wins above ~2,000. |
| C | Screen-printed spot pink | Real fluorescent pink, but a separate pass and a minimum. |
**→ Recommendation: A. Digital, CMYK, no exceptions at 300.**

### D. The pink problem — this one will surprise you
`#FF2D78` is a **screen** pink. It is outside what CMYK ink can reach. Printed digitally it comes back duller and more red. You get one of these:
- **A.** Pick a pink that CMYK can actually hit, approve it on a printed proof, and use *that* pink on Instagram too so box and feed match.
- **B.** Pay for a true spot/fluoro pink — needs screen or offset, not available at 300.
**→ Recommendation: A. Proof it on the real stock before you approve. Never approve a colour on a laptop screen.**

### E. Finish
| | Option | Cost at 300 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Matte lamination, outside only** | +₹1–2/box | **Recommended.** Unlaminated black card shows every fingerprint and scuffs in a pocket. |
| B | No lamination (raw card) | ₹0 | Feels nice for a week, then looks used. |
| C | Soft-touch / spot UV / foil / emboss | +₹3–6/box each, extra machine pass | **No. Drop 02.** |

### F. Paper stock
- **→ Recommendation: 250–270 gsm coated art card (C2S), matte laminated one side.**
- **Not 300 gsm+** — too stiff, the sleeve stops sliding over the tray.
- **Not kraft/uncoated** — looks premium in theory, but digital toner sits flat on it and the pink dies completely.

### G. What makes it collectible — pick the ones we pay for
| Lever | Cost | My call |
|---|---|---|
| **Hand-numbered 001–300** in pink pen on the inside flap | **₹0**, one evening | ✅ **Do it.** Biggest keep-value per rupee on this whole list. |
| Self-inking numbering stamp instead of pen | ~₹500–800 *(unverified — price it yourself)* | ⚪ Only if your handwriting is bad. |
| **Printed series number** — "No. 01", "No. 02", "No. 03" on the front | ₹0 | ✅ Already in the concepts. Keep. |
| **Short run on one design** — 100 only | ₹0 | ✅ Loadshedding stays the smallest. "Only 100 made" is a real line. |
| **Back panel doubles as the collector's panel** | ₹0 | ✅ Legal block + design name + "1 of 3" + one line of story. Boring text is compliant text; the story sits above it. |
| Texture (soft-touch, emboss) | ₹900–1,800 | ❌ Drop 02. |

### H. Run size — do NOT print exactly 300
Short-run printers have a minimum sheet count. **400 often costs almost the same as 300.**
**→ Recommendation: ask the price at 300 / 330 / 400, then print 330.** 30 spares covers print rejects, seeding reshoots, a press sample and the one you will drop. Printing exactly 300 means the first rejected sleeve eats a sale.

`box-concepts.md` said 200/200/100 out of 500. At **300** that becomes:

## 5. WORKING DIMENSIONS (placeholder — replace with the measured tray)

For designing only. Standard small Indian safety-match tray, **unverified**:

- Tray **≈ 50 × 35 × 15 mm**
- **Full sleeve** flat: perimeter 2×(35+15) = **100 mm** + **8 mm** glue flap = **~108 mm** around × **50 mm** tall
- **Belly band** (Option B): same **~108 mm** around × **~46 mm** tall
- Sleeve inside must be tray **+ ~0.6 mm** or it will not slide. 250 gsm card is ~0.3 mm thick and the fold eats more.
- **Nothing important within 2 mm of any edge.** Fold accuracy on a box this small is ±1 mm.
- Your two free panels: the **50 × 15 mm** sides. Don't waste them.
- Legal block: **minimum 1.0 mm cap height** (set 5–6 pt to be safe), its own zone, black on light, never over artwork.
- **"Safety Matches" must appear on the front** — the generic name, not just the brand.

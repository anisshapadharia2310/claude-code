# Seeding Plan — getting 60 boxes into the right 30 hands in Pune

**Goal:** not "give away free stuff". The goal is **30 photographs taken by other people** in 3 weeks.
Everything below is built backwards from that.

---

## THE NUMBERS

Out of the first **500 boxes**, budget **60 for seeding (12%)**.

| What | Boxes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 30 individuals — 1 box each | 30 | Your 30 target humans |
| 8 café counters — 3 boxes each | 24 | Counter presence + the owner keeps one |
| Spares (reshoots, a good chance meeting, one you drop) | 6 | You will need these |
| **Total** | **60** | Leaves 440 to sell |

At a ₹[XX] cost per box (Finance agent has the real number in T17), this is your **entire marketing budget**. That's fine. It's the cheapest marketing you'll ever run.

**Do not exceed 60.** The temptation to hand out "just one more" is how a 500-box run turns into a 380-box run.

---

## THE 30 PEOPLE — who they actually are

Don't hand boxes to friends. Friends don't post. Target by *behaviour*:

| # | Type | How to spot them | What you want back |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Café owners / baristas** | Independent places, not chains | Box lives on the counter |
| 6 | **Pune creatives** | Photographers, illustrators, designers, architecture students | A properly shot photo |
| 5 | **Micro-influencers, 2k–15k followers** | Pune food/café/lifestyle accounts. NOT big ones. | A story with a tag |
| 4 | **IT-park regulars** | People you meet at chai corners in Hinjewadi/Kharadi/Magarpatta | Word of mouth + desk presence |
| 3 | **Home/candle/décor people** | Sell candles, ceramics, incense at Pune flea markets | Cross-post to a warm audience |
| 2 | **Bartenders** | Cocktail bars — they light things professionally | Behind-the-bar visibility |
| 2 | **College culture kids** | FC Road, film/design society types | Group-chat spread |

**Why micro not macro:** a 5k-follower Pune account gets more real Pune buyers than a 200k national account, and they'll actually post for a free box. A big account will ask for money.

## WHERE TO GO — types and areas only

I have **not** invented business names. Walk these areas and pick with your eyes.

### 🟢 Koregaon Park + Mundhwa — the taste crowd
- Independent specialty-coffee roasters (Blue Tokai has a KP outlet; German Bakery is the long-standing KP institution — both appear in current Pune café listings, but **verify before you walk in**, places move).
- Small patisseries and bakehouses with outdoor seating on the tree lanes.
- Concept stores / plant shops / tattoo studios on Lane 5–7.
- **Best for:** photographers, the "this box costs ₹99 and looks like ₹400" reaction. **Start here.**

### 🟡 FC Road + Deccan Gymkhana — the volume crowd
- Old Irani-style cafés and student hangouts.
- Bookshops and stationery/art-supply shops (huge overlap with your buyer).
- Thrift + sticker + poster stalls.
- **Best for:** raw numbers, college spread, the ₹49 price point.

### 🔵 Hinjewadi (Phase 1/2/3) — the IT crowd
- The **chai tapris outside the park gates** — the ones with 30 people standing around at 4pm.
- Cafés inside/near the tech parks and the Wakad-side food streets.
- Co-working spaces — leave boxes at reception.
- **Best for:** repeat buyers with disposable income. Slowest to convert, highest lifetime value.

### 🟣 Magarpatta + Kharadi — the young-professional crowd
- Cafés around the Magarpatta City internal streets.
- The newer café clusters around Kharadi / EON free zone.
- Home-bakers and candle sellers who sell via Instagram from these townships.
- **Best for:** gifting behaviour. These people buy 3 at a time.

### ⚫ Baner / Aundh — the aesthetic crowd
- Pilates/yoga studios, plant cafés, home-décor stores.
- **Best for:** candle and incense adjacency. Safest possible product positioning.

**Order of attack:** KP first (best photos) → FC Road (volume) → Hinjewadi (money) → Magarpatta/Kharadi → Baner.

## SCRIPT 1 — walking into a café

Go at **3–5pm.** Never during lunch rush. Never at 8pm.
Ask for the **owner or the manager**. If they're not there, ask when they'll be in and come back. Don't pitch a busy barista.

Hold the box in your hand the whole time. **The box does the pitch.**

> "Hi — I'm [name], I make these here in Pune. It's a matchbox, we design the boxes ourselves.
>
> I'm not selling you anything today. I wanted to give you three — one for you, and two for the counter if you like them.
>
> People use them for the candles on the tables. And they photograph really well, which is honestly why I make them."

Then **stop talking.** Let them turn it over. They will.

**If they like it, the ask — exactly this:**
> "Two things, if you're up for it. Keep one by the till so people can see it. And if you post it, tag @[handle] — I'll repost you."

**If they ask about stocking it:**
> "Yes — I'll come back with a proper price for you next week."
Then tell the Launch agent (T15 is the retail pitch script). **Don't negotiate wholesale on the spot.** You'll undersell yourself.

**If they say no:** "No worries at all — keep one anyway." Leave one box. Walk out. That one box still ends up on a counter half the time.

**Never say:** "it's for smokers", "for the smoking area", "your customers who smoke". Not once. See the tobacco note below.

## SCRIPT 2 — the chai corner outside an IT park

The tapri at 4pm, Hinjewadi or Kharadi. Groups of 4–6 people standing with cutting chai.

**⚠️ Read this first.** Yes, some of these people smoke. **You are not there for that and you will never say it.** You're a person who makes a thing in Pune, showing it to people over chai. If you ever frame this as smoker outreach — in person, in a caption, in a DM — you've created a legal problem for a ₹99 product. Not worth it. Ever.

Walk up to the group, not to one person. Keep it under 30 seconds.

> "Sorry — quick one. I make these in Pune."
>
> *(hand one box to the person who looks most interested — it'll get passed around on its own)*
>
> "They're matchboxes. We do the designs ourselves. That one's yours."
>
> "If you post it, tag @[handle]. That's the whole ask."

Then **leave**. Don't hover. The box gets passed around the group for five minutes after you go — that's the actual moment that works, and you standing there kills it.

**One box per group, not one per person.** Scarcity within the group makes them talk about it. Six boxes handed out = six people quietly pocketing them.

**Best version of this:** hit the same tapri twice, a week apart. The second time someone will recognise the box. That's your first real customer.

## SCRIPT 3 — the DM to a micro-influencer

Short. No pitch deck energy.

> Hey — I make collectible matchboxes here in Pune, we design the boxes ourselves.
> Can I send you one? No cost, no obligation to post.
> Just need an address.

That's it. Send nothing else. If they reply asking about the brand, *then* send the link.

**When you post the box, one line:**
> Only if you like it — tag @[handle]. Nothing else needed.

**Never ask for a Reel.** Ask for nothing and you get stories. Ask for a Reel and you get ghosted.

---

## THE EXACT ASK — memorise these two lines
1. **"Keep it on the counter."** (cafés, shops, co-working receptions)
2. **"If you post it, tag @[handle] — I'll repost you."** (everyone, always)

Never ask for more than two things. Never ask for a review, a rating, a story *and* a post. One ask lands; three asks get ignored.

**Always say the reposting part out loud.** Free reach on your account is the actual payment you're offering, and people want it.

---

## WHAT TO WRITE ON THE BOX YOU HAND OVER
Take 5 seconds with a white paint pen or a small sticker on the back:

> `seeded · [initials] · [date]`

Costs nothing, makes the box feel numbered and given rather than dumped, and it makes tracking trivially easy when a photo shows up.

1. Which **area** is posting most? → go back there.
2. Which **design** is getting photographed? → print more of that in Drop 02.
3. Who hasn't posted after 10 days? → one nudge, then let it go.

## THE ONE FOLLOW-UP (only one, ever)
10 days after giving a box, if nothing:
> Hey! No pressure at all — just checking the box reached you okay?

That's the whole message. **Don't mention posting.** Roughly a third of them will post right after this, purely out of guilt. Never send a second nudge — it turns a warm person cold.

---

## WHAT GOOD LOOKS LIKE (set the bar now)
From 60 seeded boxes over 3 weeks, expect:
- **12–18 posts/stories** from other people (20–30% is a normal, good seeding conversion)
- **4–6 café counters** holding boxes
- **2–3 cafés** asking about stocking
- **1 unexpectedly good one** — a photographer or a mid-size account posts it and you get 40 orders in a day

If you get **fewer than 8 posts from 60 boxes**, the problem isn't the plan — it's either the box design or the ask. Change the ask first. It's cheaper.

---

## ⚠️ TWO THINGS THAT WILL SINK THIS
1. **Tobacco framing.** Once. In one DM. That's all it takes. The brand is fire, art and collecting — candles, diyas, cake, incense, camping. Hold the line in person exactly as hard as you hold it in captions.
2. **Seeding more than 60.** Every free box past 60 is a box you can't sell, and giving away 100 boxes doesn't get you twice the posts — it just makes the box feel free. Scarcity is the product.

# Drop 01 — 500 Boxes. How They Actually Get Sold.

Written 12 Sep 2026. Pune only. Total capital **₹20,000**.

---

## THE ONE NUMBER

> **Do not place the ₹8,000 production order until 60 boxes are PRE-PAID.**

Not "interested". Not "I'll take one bro". **Money received. UPI screenshot in hand.**

### Why 60

| Check | Number |
|---|---|
| Cash in from 60 pre-paid boxes @ ₹99 | **₹5,940** |
| Cost of the production run | **₹8,000** |
| Your own money actually at risk | **₹2,060** |
| % of the 500 sold before a box exists | **12%** |
| Untouched capital still in the bank | **₹12,000** |

60 is the number where the worst case stops being scary.
If the whole thing dies after this, you lost **₹2,060**, not ₹8,000.

### The anti-fooling-yourself rule

Of those 60 boxes, at least **25 must be from people who are NOT in your phone contacts.**

Friends buy once out of love. Strangers buy because the product is good.
If 55 of your 60 are friends, you have **not** proven demand. Keep pushing before you order.

### The hard gates (write these on your wall)

- [ ] **< 60 pre-paid** → do NOT place the order. Extend pre-orders by 1 week.
- [ ] **60+ pre-paid** → place the ₹8,000 order the same day.
- [ ] **₹3,000 ad money stays locked until 150 boxes are sold and paid for.** No exceptions.

## IF PRE-ORDERS FLOP

**Trigger: fewer than 60 pre-paid by 5 Oct.**

Do not order. Do not "just do a small run anyway". Do not touch the ad money.

**Diagnose first. There are only three reasons:**

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Lots of views, no clicks | The product doesn't look good enough | Reshoot. Better photos, a real hand, real light. |
| Clicks, no payment | Price or trust problem | Add a ₹49 single. Add your face + phone number on the page. |
| No views at all | Nobody knows you exist | Distribution problem, not product problem. Go offline. |

**The 14-day rescue (costs ₹0):**

- [ ] Extend pre-orders 2 weeks. Say it publicly: "not enough yet, being honest with you."
- [ ] Reshoot the product. Bad photos kill more launches than bad products.
- [ ] Add a **₹49 single-box** entry price. Lower the wall.
- [ ] Go physical: **15 cafés, 3 colleges, 2 co-working spaces.** Carry a sample. Collect money by UPI on the spot.
- [ ] Pre-sell to cafés: ask one venue to **pre-pay ₹1,200 for 20 boxes.** One yes is 20 boxes.
- [ ] Ask the 10 warmest supporters to each bring **2 buyers**. That alone is 20 boxes.

**Then re-check at day 14:**

- **60+** → order. You lost 2 weeks, nothing else.
- **35–59** → ask the supplier for a **250-box run** instead. Smaller order, ~₹4,500, lower risk. Re-price accordingly.
- **Under 35** → **STOP.** Do not print. You still have **₹20,000**. Change the product, the price, or the audience — then try again. Stopping here is a win, not a failure.

## IF IT SELLS OUT IN 3 DAYS

Happy problem. It is still a problem. Handle it like this:

**Do NOT:**
- Do not panic-order Drop 02 at 3am.
- Do not promise a date you cannot hit.
- Do not eat into the **50 reserve**. It is not stock.

**DO, in this order:**

1. **Close the store the moment you hit 450 sold.** Say "Drop 01 sold out" loudly. Sold-out is the best ad you will ever run — and it is free.
2. **Open a waitlist, not a pre-order.** Email/WhatsApp only, no money taken. Target **200 names**.
3. **Raise the price on Drop 02.** If 500 vanished in 3 days at ₹99, the price was too low. Test **₹129**, or a 3-box set at **₹299**.
4. **Call the supplier same week.** Ask for a **1,000-box** quote and the real lead time. Get it in writing.
5. **Fund Drop 02 only from Drop 01 cash.** Roughly ₹25,000 in means a ~1,000-box run at ~₹14,000 is affordable without touching the ₹20,000. Keep the ₹5,500 reserve untouched either way.
6. **Then** spend the ₹3,000 on ads — pointing at the **waitlist**, not at an empty store.
7. **Ration the cafés.** If you sold out fast, cafés get 10 boxes each, not 30. Being hard to get is the brand.

**The trap:** selling out in 3 days at ₹99 with ₹16 costs feels like a win, but if you never tested a higher price you left money on the table for all of Drop 02 and Drop 03. **Test the price while you have the leverage.**

# Trademark Notes — for a tiny matchbox brand

**Written 12 Sep 2026. Links checked 12 Sep 2026.**

## The honest headline

**Do not spend ₹4,500 on a trademark this month.**

Do the **free search** before you print a single box. That part is urgent.
The **filing** can wait until you have sold a few hundred boxes.

The expensive mistake is not "failed to register".
The expensive mistake is **printing 500 boxes with a name someone else already owns**.

---

## Which class? **Class 34. Confirmed.**

Trademarks are filed per "class" of goods. There are 45.

**Matches are in Class 34.** The official Nice Classification heading for Class 34 is:
> **"Tobacco; smokers' articles; matches."**

The word *matches* is literally in the class heading. There is no ambiguity here.
Source: https://go.trademarkia.com/trademark-class-34 (seen 12 Sep 2026)
Source: https://www.trademarksindia.net/trademark-classes/class-34-tobacco-products-smokers-articles.php (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**It is NOT Class 4.** Class 4 is industrial oils, fuels, illuminants, **candles and wicks**.
People get this wrong because both are "things that make fire". Candles = 4. Matches = 34.

**Slightly awkward fact worth knowing:** Class 34 is mostly a *tobacco* class.
Your brand will sit on a register next to cigarette brands. That is normal and fine —
it is where every match brand in the world lives. But be aware if you are marketing
hard to a young audience: some ad platforms treat Class 34 goods with suspicion.

**If you later sell candles or incense too**, that is a **different class** and a
**separate ₹4,500**. Fees are per class, per mark. Plan the brand accordingly.

## What it costs — real numbers

| Who you are | Government fee, e-filing, per class |
|---|---|
| **Individual, or Startup, or Small Enterprise (Udyam/MSME)** | **₹4,500** |
| Company, LLP, anyone larger | ₹9,000 |

Source: https://www.intepat.com/blog/trademark-registration-fees-india (seen 12 Sep 2026)
Source: https://disytax.com/trademark-fees-india/ (seen 12 Sep 2026)
Form is **TM-A**, filed online at https://ipindiaonline.gov.in/trademarkefiling/user/frmLoginNew.aspx

**You qualify for the ₹4,500 rate.** You are an individual, and once you do your free
Udyam registration you are also a Small Enterprise. Either route gets you the lower fee.

> **This is the second reason to do Udyam this week. It is worth ₹4,500 to you.**
> See `legal-checklist.md` item 2.

**Other costs to be honest about:**
- **A lawyer/agent to file it for you:** typically ₹1,500–5,000 on top. Optional.
  You *can* file it yourself on the IP India portal. It is fiddly but doable.
- **The fee is non-refundable.** If your mark is rejected for similarity, the money is gone
  and you pay again for a new application. **This is exactly why the free search comes first.**
- **Renewal:** registration lasts 10 years, then must be renewed.
  **VERIFY WITH A CA/IP agent for the current renewal fee.** Not your problem for a decade.

## How long it takes

| Stage | Time |
|---|---|
| File TM-A → you can use the **™** symbol | **Immediately** |
| Examination report | 3–12 months |
| If objected: you reply (30 days) + possible hearing | adds months |
| Published in the journal → opposition window | **4 months, fixed** |
| Certificate issued | 1–3 months after that |
| **Total, no problems** | **roughly 12–18 months** |

Source: https://origiin.com/trademark-registration-timeline-india-2026/ (seen 12 Sep 2026)
Source: https://www.registerkaro.in/post/time-period-for-trademark-registration-in-india (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**The useful bit:** you can legally put **™** next to your brand name **the day you file**.
You can only use **®** once it is actually registered.
Many small brands use ™ for years and never file. That is a real, common choice.

## The free search tool — and exactly how to use it

**Official IP India public search (free, no login):**
**https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/**

Backup mirror: https://ipindiaservices.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/frmmain.aspx

### Step by step — 10 minutes per name

1. Open the link above.
2. Choose search type **Wordmark**.
3. In **Search Type**, pick **"Contains"** (not "Start With" — too narrow).
4. In **Wordmark**, type your candidate name.
5. In **Class**, type **34**.
6. Enter the captcha. Click Search.
7. **Then repeat the whole thing with search type "Phonetic".**

**Phonetic is the one that saves you.** The Registry rejects names that *sound* like
existing ones, not just names spelled identically. "Jwala" and "Jwaala" collide.

### How to read the results

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Zero results in Class 34** | Good sign. Not a guarantee — but the best free signal you can get. |
| Result marked **"Registered"** | Someone owns it. **Pick another name.** |
| Result marked **"Objected" / "Opposed" / "Abandoned"** | Messy. Abandoned may be reusable. Ask an IP agent before betting on it. |
| Result marked **"Formalities Chk Pass" / "Advertised"** | Someone filed before you. You lose. Pick another name. |
| A near-identical name in a **different** class | Usually survivable — but if it is a **famous** brand, don't. See below. |

### Also search these, because they are free
- **Class 4** as well (candles/fuels) — adjacent goods, same "fire" idea.
- Plain **Google + Instagram + a domain registrar**, for the same name.
  A registered trademark is not the only way to get into trouble. An existing
  unregistered business with real customers can still stop you (passing off).

**I could not run these searches for you from this machine** — the IP India site is
blocked from here, and it needs a captcha anyway. **You have to do this yourself.**
It genuinely takes 10 minutes per name.

## Obvious collision red flags — check these before you fall in love with a name

**Existing Indian match brands you must not be confusable with.** ITC alone markets
**Aim, Ship, Homelites, Cheeta Fight and Kapas** in safety matches.
Source: https://www.itcportal.com/businesses/fmcg/safety-matches.aspx (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**Avoid anything that is a near-miss on those.** Not "Shipp", not "Aym", not "Cheetah",
not "Home Light". ITC has lawyers and you have ₹20,000.

**Other red flags to run past the search:**

1. **Generic fire words.** Agni, Jwala, Flame, Spark, Blaze, Fire, Light, Deep, Diya.
   These are crowded in Classes 4 and 34. Expect collisions. Search each one properly.
2. **Any famous brand in any class.** If a name is a well-known mark, class doesn't protect
   you. Do not use a big name "but for matches".
3. **Descriptive names.** "Safety Match Co", "Pune Matches", "Best Matches" — a purely
   descriptive name is hard to register at all. You want something distinctive/invented.
4. **Surnames and place names.** Harder to register. Avoid if you can.
5. **Anything tobacco-adjacent.** You are already in a tobacco class. A name that implies
   smoking will cause you grief with Instagram ads and Amazon listings, even if legal.
6. **Foreign brands.** Check the name isn't a live matchbox/lighter brand in another country.
   Google it in quotes with the word "matches".
7. **The .in / .com domain and the Instagram handle.** If both are taken by an active
   business, the name is functionally unavailable even if the register is clear.

## The cheap and smart sequence — do it in this order

**Do not skip steps and do not reorder them.**

### Step 1 — Shortlist 5 names (Brand agent is on this, task T8)
Not 1. Not 10. **Five.** You will lose 2–3 of them to collisions.

### Step 2 — Free trademark search. TODAY. ₹0.
Run each of the 5 through **Wordmark + Phonetic** in **Class 34**.
Kill any name with a registered or pending hit. Do not argue with the result.

### Step 3 — Google + Instagram check. ₹0.
Search the name in quotes. Check the Instagram handle. Check `.in` and `.com`.
If an active Indian business already uses it, move on.

### Step 4 — Buy the domain and grab the Instagram handle. ~₹500–900.
**As soon as a name survives steps 2 and 3, lock the handle and the domain the same day.**
This is the cheapest defensive move you will ever make. Handles get taken.
(Website agent has ₹500 budgeted for the domain.)

### Step 5 — Start using the name with a ™ next to it. ₹0.
You get some common-law rights in India simply by **using** a mark in trade and
building a reputation. Keep dated evidence: your first invoices, your first Instagram
posts, your first café order. **Screenshot and save them in one folder.**
That folder is genuinely useful if anyone ever challenges you.

### Step 6 — Do Udyam registration. ₹0.
This is what makes your future filing cost ₹4,500 instead of ₹9,000.

### Step 7 — File TM-A only when the business earns it.
**My suggested trigger: file once you have sold roughly 500 boxes, or the moment
a stockist/marketplace asks for the trademark, whichever comes first.**

**File earlier than that if:** you find your name is getting copied, or you are about
to spend real money on packaging/ads, or a café chain wants an exclusive deal.

## What you actually get for ₹4,500 — and what you don't

**You get:** the exclusive right to that name for matches in India, the ® symbol, the
ability to stop a copycat, and something a marketplace or investor takes seriously.

**You don't get:** protection in other classes, protection abroad, or any automatic
enforcement. **A trademark is a weapon, not a shield — someone still has to pick it up
and use it, and that someone is you, with a lawyer, at your cost.**

At ₹0–2 lakh revenue, a ₹4,500 trademark is a reasonable but **optional** spend.
The ₹0 search is **not** optional.

## My read for the founder

**Safest trademark candidates from this list: UJED and PINKHEAD.** Both are distinctive
rather than descriptive, which is exactly what makes a mark registrable *and* defensible.

**KAADEPETI is the strongest brand and a weaker trademark.** That's a real trade-off,
not a mistake — you can absolutely run it with a ™ and register the **logo** instead of
the word. **Ask an IP agent about filing it as a device/logo mark.** That is the standard
workaround for a descriptive name and it is the same ₹4,500.

**Drop PHULJHADI and BURN BOOK before you get attached to them.**

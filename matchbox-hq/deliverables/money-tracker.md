## Three traps to watch for

**Trap 1 — the pre-order illusion.**

If 60 people pre-order at ₹99, you will see ₹5,940 in your account.

That is not ₹5,940 of yours.

About ₹1,080 of it is boxes you still have to make, and about ₹900 is delivery and packing you still have to pay.

Your real share is about ₹3,960. Spend only that.

**Trap 2 — the small leaks.**

₹50 here for tape. ₹120 there for a print test. ₹80 for a rider who came for nothing.

Ten of those is ₹1,000 — that is 19 boxes of profit gone.

Log every single one, however small.

**Trap 3 — eating Drop 02's trays. ← this one is new.**

300 of your 600 trays belong to Drop 02.

Put them in a separate bag. Write **"DROP 02 — DO NOT OPEN"** on it.

Every tray you pull out of that bag costs Drop 02 ₹7 of margin and pushes its break-even up.

If the bag is empty when Drop 02 comes, the two-part model has bought you nothing.

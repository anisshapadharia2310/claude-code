# Legal Checklist — Matchbox Brand, Pune

**Written 12 Sep 2026. Every link below was opened on 12 Sep 2026.**

Read this first:

You are a **reseller**, not a manufacturer.
You buy finished safety matches from a Sivakasi factory and sell them in your own printed box.
That makes your legal life **much** simpler than you probably fear.

**The one-line answer:** in week 1 you need almost nothing, and almost all of it is free.

> **Honesty rule I followed:** where I could not confirm something from a government or
> solid current source, I wrote **VERIFY WITH A CA**. I have not guessed at fees.
> I am not a lawyer. A Pune CA will charge you ₹500–1,500 for a 20-minute sanity check.

---

## PART 1 — WEEK 1. FREE THINGS. DO THIS.

### 1. PAN card — **you almost certainly already have this**
**What it is:** your personal tax number.
**Do you need it?** Yes. Everything else hangs off it.
**Cost:** ₹0 if you have one. ~₹107 to apply fresh.
**Time:** already done, or ~15 days.
**Where:** https://www.onlineservices.nsdl.com/paam/endUserRegisterContact.html
**Documents:** Aadhaar.

**Important:** as a sole proprietor, **your personal PAN is the business's PAN**.
You do not get a separate PAN for the brand. That is normal.

---

### 2. Udyam (MSME) registration — **DO THIS. IT IS FREE AND TAKES 10 MINUTES.**
**What it is:** a free government certificate that says "I am a small business."
**Do you need it?** Yes. This is the single best-value thing on this list.

Why it matters to you specifically:
- It is the closest thing to "proof my business exists" when a café or supplier asks.
- **It cuts your trademark fee in half later** (₹4,500 instead of ₹9,000 — see `trademark-notes.md`).
- Traders (buy-and-resell, exactly what you do) have been eligible since 2021.

**Cost:** ₹0. Free on the official site.
**Time:** 10–15 minutes. Certificate is instant.
**Where (ONLY this URL):** https://udyamregistration.gov.in
**Documents:** Aadhaar number + a mobile linked to it for OTP. PAN. Bank account + IFSC.
No uploads. It is self-declaration.

> **SCAM WARNING.** Dozens of sites look like the real one and charge ₹500–2,000.
> The real site ends in **.gov.in**. If a site asks you to pay, close it.
> Source: https://udyamregistration.gov.in (seen 12 Sep 2026) — registration is free there.

---

### 3. A separate bank account — **DO THIS IN WEEK 1**
**What it is:** a bank account that is only for the matchbox money.
**Do you need it?** Yes, practically. Not a law, but you will lose your mind without it.

**Start simple:** a **second savings account** is fine for month 1 and costs nothing extra.
Banks technically want a **current account** for business, and will ask for Udyam + GST.
Current accounts often need ₹5,000–25,000 minimum balance — **that money is dead to you.**

**My recommendation:** keep a separate savings account + a separate UPI ID for now.
Open a current account only when a customer or Razorpay forces you to.
**VERIFY WITH A CA** — banks vary and some push current accounts hard.

**Cost:** ₹0 if you use a second savings account.
**Time:** 1 day online with most banks.

---

### 4. Shops & Establishment ("Gumasta") — **DO THIS. FREE.**
**What it is:** telling Maharashtra "I have started a business at this address."
**Do you need it?** Yes, if you are running the business from a place in Pune.

**Here is the good part.** Under the **Maharashtra Shops and Establishments Act, 2017**,
if you employ **fewer than 10 workers** you do NOT need a full licence.
You only file an **online intimation in Form F**. You get a receipt. That's it.

**Cost:** ₹0 for the under-10-workers intimation.
Source: https://www.deasra.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/shops-and-establishment-act.pdf (seen 12 Sep 2026)
and https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/shop-and-establishment-license-not-required-for-less-then-10-employees.html (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**Time:** must be filed within **60 days** of starting business.
**Where:** Maharashtra Labour department portal — https://mahakamgar.maharashtra.gov.in
**Documents:** Aadhaar of owner, and a photo of the premises showing a **name board in Marathi**.

> Yes, the Marathi name board is a real requirement in Maharashtra. Plan for it on your
> door/shelf even if you work from home. **VERIFY WITH A CA** how strictly it is applied
> to a home-based online seller — enforcement differs.

---

### 5. Explosives / match-specific licence — **YOU DO NOT NEED THIS.**

This is the question that scares every matchbox founder. Here is the honest answer.

**Ordinary safety matches are not treated like fireworks.**
The heavy licensing under the **Explosives Act, 1884** and **Explosives Rules, 2008**
(PESO licences, Form LE-5 shopkeeper licences, storage magazines) is aimed at
**explosives and fireworks** — including **Colour/Star matches**, which ARE classed as
Class 7 Fireworks.
Source: https://peso.gov.in/web/en/explosives-act-1884 (seen 12 Sep 2026).

**Plain safety matches in a box are sold openly in every kirana shop in India
without a PESO licence.** The licensing burden sits on the **Sivakasi factory**, not on you.

**What you must do instead — and this one is genuinely important:**
> **Ask your supplier for a copy of their licence/BIS papers and keep it on file.**
> You are buying from them. If the product is dodgy, it becomes your problem.
> Ask: *"Do you make to IS 2653? Can you send your BIS licence number?"*

**IS 2653:2004 "Safety Matches in Boxes"** is the Indian Standard for this product.
Source: https://www.services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/bisconnect/standard_review/Standard_review/Isdetails?ID=OTE3NA%3D%3D (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**VERIFY WITH A CA (or a Pune Legal Metrology / PESO officer):** I could not open the PESO
FAQ PDF from this machine — it was blocked. I am confident a reseller of plain safety
matches needs no explosives licence, but if you ever move to **colour/star/scented/novelty**
matches, that answer changes and you must re-check.

**A separate, real problem:** matches are **restricted for air cargo and some couriers**.
That is a shipping problem, not a licence problem. The Launch agent is on it.

---

### 6. FSSAI food licence — **NOT NEEDED. IGNORE.**
Matches are not food. FSSAI does not apply to you. Move on.

## PART 2 — ONLY WHEN YOU CROSS THIS LINE

### 7. GST registration — **the one that actually matters**

**What it is:** a tax number that lets you charge and pay GST.

**You do NOT need GST right now if ALL of these are true:**
- You sell only inside Maharashtra, AND
- Your turnover is under ₹40 lakh a year, AND
- You are not selling through Amazon / Flipkart / Blinkit.

**The ₹40 lakh number:** Maharashtra is a normal-category state. The threshold for a
person supplying **only goods** is **₹40 lakh**. For services it is ₹20 lakh.
Sources (seen 12 Sep 2026):
https://cleartax.in/s/gst-registration-limits-increased
https://tallysolutions.com/gst/gst-limit-registration-threshold-india/

> **Trap:** the ₹40 lakh limit is only for someone supplying **goods exclusively**.
> The moment you also sell a *service* (design work, a paid workshop, consulting),
> you drop to **₹20 lakh for your whole turnover**. At ₹0–2 lakh revenue this does not
> bite you, but remember it.

**You DO need GST immediately — regardless of turnover — if any of these happen:**

| Trigger | Why |
|---|---|
| You ship a box to a customer **outside Maharashtra** | Inter-state supply of goods = compulsory registration under s.24 CGST Act |
| You list on **Amazon / Flipkart / Myntra** | They will not activate a seller account without a GSTIN |
| You want to claim **input tax credit** on your Sivakasi purchase | No GSTIN, no credit |
| A café/retailer demands a **GST invoice** | Many will. Small cafés often won't care. |

**The marketplace trigger, explained honestly.**
Section 24 of the CGST Act says anyone supplying through an e-commerce operator must register.
**BUT** Notification 34/2023 (in force from **1 Oct 2023**) created an exemption:
an unregistered person can sell **goods** through an e-commerce operator **without GST** if
they sell in **only one state**, make **no inter-state supply**, have a PAN, and get an
**enrolment number** on the GST portal.
Source: https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/notification-exemption-gst-registration-eco-suppliers.html (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**In practice, though: Amazon and Flipkart still ask for a GSTIN** for taxable goods
and will not switch your account on without one.
Source: https://easy-vyapar.in/blog/gst-registration-ecommerce-amazon-flipkart (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**So the real rule for you is:** *your own website, Pune only = no GST.
Amazon/Flipkart = get GST first.*

**Cost:** ₹0 to register on the government portal.
**Ongoing cost:** this is the bit people forget. Once registered you must file returns
**every month or quarter, even for ₹0 sales**. A CA charges roughly ₹500–1,500/month.
**That is the real reason not to register before you need to.**
**VERIFY WITH A CA** — get a quote from a Pune CA before you register.

**Time:** usually 7–15 working days. Aadhaar authentication speeds it up.
**Where:** https://www.gst.gov.in
**Documents:** PAN, Aadhaar, photo, proof of business address (electricity bill + NOC
from the owner if it's your parents' flat), bank account proof, Udyam certificate.

**GST rate on matches — VERIFY WITH A CA BEFORE YOU PRINT AN MRP.**
Matches sit under **HSN 3605**. Older sources say handmade safety matches 5% and other
matches 18%. The **56th GST Council (3 Sep 2025)** scrapped the 12% slab from
**22 Sep 2025**, so the rate for matches has very likely moved.
Sources (seen 12 Sep 2026):
https://cleartax.in/s/matches-gst-rates-hsn-code-3605
https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/press_release_press_information_bureau.pdf
**I could not confirm today's exact rate for HSN 3605. Ask the Sivakasi supplier what
rate is on their invoice — that is the fastest true answer.** The Finance agent needs this too.

### 8. Legal Metrology "packer" registration — **the sneaky one nobody tells you about**

**What it is:** a registration you file because you are putting a **branded, pre-packed
product with an MRP** into the market.

**Do you need it?** **Probably yes — this is the one real licence in your whole plan.**

Here is why it catches you. Under **Rule 6(1)(a)** of the Legal Metrology (Packaged
Commodities) Rules, 2011, if your brand name and address appear on the box as the
**marketer / brand owner**, you become the **"deemed manufacturer"** and you carry the
liability for what's printed on that box.
Source: https://1-comply.com/faqs-on-legal-metrology-act-and-packaged-commodities-rules/ (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**Cost:** **₹500** registration fee in Maharashtra (₹100 to alter it later).
Source: https://www.vaidhmapan.maharashtra.gov.in/HomePages/newRegCert.html (seen 12 Sep 2026)
and https://www.diligencecertification.com/rule-27-legal-metrology-act-2009/ (seen 12 Sep 2026)

**Time:** a few weeks, varies by office.
**Where:** Maharashtra Legal Metrology — https://www.vaidhmapan.maharashtra.gov.in
**Documents:** PAN, address proof, Udyam certificate, a **sample label / box artwork**.

**When to actually do it:** once your printed boxes exist and you are selling them
publicly. Not before. But budget the ₹500 now.

**VERIFY WITH A CA / the Pune Legal Metrology office** — whether the Sivakasi factory's
own registration covers you, or whether you need your own as the brand owner. My best
understanding is that **the brand owner named on the pack needs their own registration**,
but the two of you may be able to share the burden if the factory is named as manufacturer.
**One phone call to the Pune Legal Metrology office settles this. Make it.**

## PART 3 — IGNORE FOR NOW

| Thing | Why you can ignore it |
|---|---|
| **FSSAI licence** | Matches are not food. Never applies. |
| **Private Limited company** | Costs ₹6,000–15,000 + annual filings. A sole proprietorship is fine at ₹0–2 lakh. |
| **Import Export Code (IEC)** | Only if you export. You don't. |
| **PESO / explosives licence** | Factory's problem, not yours (see item 5). |
| **Trade licence from PMC** | Generally aimed at shops/premises. **VERIFY WITH A CA** if you open a physical outlet. |
| **Professional Tax** | Maharashtra PT usually kicks in with employees/registration. You have none. **VERIFY WITH A CA** once you register for anything. |
| **Trademark filing** | Not until you've sold a few hundred boxes. |
| **BIS licence** | That is the *manufacturer's* certification, not the reseller's. Ask your supplier for theirs. |

## YOUR ACTUAL WEEK-1 LIST

1. [ ] Go to https://udyamregistration.gov.in and register. **Free. 15 minutes. Do it today.**
2. [ ] Open a second savings account + a dedicated UPI ID.
3. [ ] File Shops & Establishment **Form F** intimation on https://mahakamgar.maharashtra.gov.in
4. [ ] Run the free trademark search (see `trademark-notes.md`) before you print anything.
5. [ ] Ask your Sivakasi supplier: *"Do you make to IS 2653? Send me your BIS licence number and your GST invoice format."*

and buys you nothing until you sell outside Maharashtra or list on Amazon.

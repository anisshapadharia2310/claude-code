## 2. WHY THIS IS ITS OWN JOB

- **Different failure mode, and it is the friendlier one.** A bad post dies in 24 hours and you post again tomorrow. **Packaging has no undo; this has nothing but undo.** So this meeting can move fast and be wrong.
- **Different deadline, and it is the nearest one.** Day 1 is **Mon 14 Sep — two days away.** But it needs **zero decisions from you** to start: Week 1 is already written and paste-ready. Packaging needs decisions and has a printer waiting. That is why packaging goes first and this goes second.
- **Different money: ₹0.** Ads are locked until pre-orders exist. Everything here costs a phone and an evening.
- **Different skill.** Framing, sound, and posting on a day you don't feel like it. Nothing here is measured in millimetres.

---

## 3. DECISIONS YOU MUST MAKE

### A. Start the account now, or wait for the name?
- **A.** Create the account **today** under a placeholder handle, post from Day 1, rename it after **Fri 25 Sep**. Instagram lets you change a handle, and at under 1,000 followers you lose nothing.
- **B.** Wait for the name. Lose **11 days** of Week 1 tease content that needs no name at all.

**→ Recommendation: A.** Week 1 is literally designed to work without a name. Waiting is the expensive option.

### B. Are you on camera?
- **A.** **Hands and voice only** for Weeks 1–2, face from Week 3.
- **B.** Face from Day 1.
- **C.** Never on camera.

**→ Recommendation: A.** Hands-only is the easiest thing to shoot and the best-performing format for a small object. But **C loses you the build-in-public audience** — the founder's face is why people back a ₹99 box from a stranger. Week 3 is the right time, once there is something real to hold.

### C. Do you say ₹99 before the name is locked?
- **A.** Yes — price it publicly from **Day 10**.
- **B.** Hold the price until the reveal.

**→ Recommendation: A.** The price filters out people who were never buying, and ₹99 is a *good* number for what this looks like. Saying it early makes the pre-order ask feel routine instead of sudden.

### D. Reels vs carousels — what's the weekly mix?
- **Reels find strangers.** They are the only thing that reaches people who don't follow you.
- **Carousels convince them.** Saves come from carousels. A save is a person who intends to buy later.
- **Stories sell.** Link stickers, polls and DMs live here. Stories are where the waitlist actually fills.

**→ Recommendation per week: 3 Reels · 2 carousels · 2 photos, plus stories most days.**
Do not chase a daily Reel. Three good Reels beat seven rushed ones, and rushed Reels are why people quit in Week 2.

### E. Language
- **→ Recommendation: English captions, Marathi as the punchline.** "लाईट गेली." lands as a graphic, not as a paragraph. Full-Marathi captions cut your Hinjewadi/Kharadi reach — half that crowd isn't from Maharashtra.

### F. ⚠️ Week 4 of the existing calendar is now wrong — fix it in this meeting
`instagram-30-days.md` has you **packing orders and posting "SOLD OUT"** in Week 4 (5–13 Oct). **The boxes don't exist until ~24 Oct.** You cannot film packing something you don't have.
**→ Recommendation: rewrite Week 4 as "the making weeks."** Printer proof, the sleeves arriving, the Sivakasi story, hand-numbering 001–300, the Drop 02 tease. Waiting is content. Faking delivery is not.

## 4. WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU

### 🎬 The shoot day — **Sat 13 or Sun 14 Sep, 90 minutes**
This single session is the highest-leverage thing in the whole launch. **90 minutes = 3 weeks of posts.** Buy a ₹2 kirana matchbox on the way home; that's your stand-in.

Shot list — bring it back and we'll cut it:
- [ ] **Dark room strike** — lights fully off, phone propped, **5 takes.** Lock exposure BEFORE you strike or the flare wrecks it. No music, just the sound.
- [ ] **ASMR scrape** — phone 10 cm away, 4 takes. The scrape, the catch, the crackle.
- [ ] **Hands-only open** — thumb pushes the drawer, one match out. 3 takes.
- [ ] **Kirana shelf** — the wall of ₹2 boxes. Wide + one close-up label.
- [ ] **Pune signboards** — 5 shots of good Devanagari type, shot straight-on.
- [ ] **Your desk** — sketches, a ruler, chai. Messy is the point.
- [ ] **Chai + candle flat-lay**, dark table, warm light.
- [ ] **A lit candle, a diya, incense, a birthday cake** — the whole "what we light instead" set, in one go.

**Phone rules:** 9:16 · clean the lens · lock exposure · 4 takes keep 1 · shoot near a window by day, full dark by night.

### 🎬 Two more shoots, diarised now
- **~Fri 25 Sep** — the day the Sivakasi samples land. First real product footage. Tray only, no sleeve yet.
- **~Fri 10 Oct** — the day the printed sleeves land. **This is the real reveal.** Clear the evening.

### Only you can do these — nobody can cover for you
- [ ] **50 hand-written DMs** to real people. Not a broadcast. This fills the waitlist, not the algorithm.
- [ ] **The face-to-camera "why I made this"** — one per week from Week 3. 40 seconds, no script.
- [ ] **The Q&A story** (Day 20) — answer the money questions honestly, including what it cost.
- [ ] **Replying to every single DM and comment for 30 days.** At this size, replies *are* the marketing.
- [ ] **The brand name** (D-002) — the reveal post and every "link in bio" is stuck behind it.
- [ ] **Say yes or no to the 60 seeded boxes** going to the 30 people in `seeding-plan.md`. Week 3's UGC content is entirely made of other people's photos. No seeding, no Week 3.

### The things we are NOT doing — say yes to this too
- No paid ads until pre-orders exist (₹3,000 is locked).
- No cigarettes, bidis, ashtrays, rolling paper, "sutta", "smoke break" — **not even blurred in a corner of a reposted photo.** One screenshot costs you café partnerships and marketplace listings.
- No reposting UGC without checking every frame first.

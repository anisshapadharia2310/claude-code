## 🧱 IF A STAGE IS STUCK — the one fix for each

| Stuck stage | The single fix |
|---|---|
| Waitlist flat | Post the object, not your feelings about the object. Better photos. |
| Waitlist big, nobody pays | Add a deadline. "Pre-orders close Sunday" moves more than any discount. |
| Cafés say "leave your number" | You did not hand them the box in 15 seconds. Do it earlier. |
| Cafés stock but don't reorder | Wrong venue type. Move to specialty coffee + bars with terraces. |
| Creators ignore you | DM is too long or too needy. **40 words. No attachments. No deck.** |
| Creators take the box, never post | Stop sending unless they reply first. Every time. |

# The ₹3,000 Pune Ads Plan

**Locked until earned. Read the gate before you read anything else.**

---

## 🔒 THE GATE

**Do not spend one rupee on ads until ALL THREE are true:**

- [ ] **150 boxes sold AND paid for** (pre-orders + café wholesale + DM sales)
- [ ] **At least 1 café has REORDERED** (not just stocked — reordered)
- [ ] **The 3-box set at ₹249 is live on the site**

Miss any one → **the ₹3,000 stays in the bank.**

**Why:** ads do not create demand. They pour petrol on a fire that is already lit. If 150 people haven't paid you without ads, ₹3,000 will buy you nothing but a lesson you could have had for free.

## THE MATHS — DO THIS FIRST

### What one order is actually worth to you

**Cost per box: ₹16** (₹8,000 ÷ 500)

| | **1 box @ ₹99** | **2 boxes @ ₹198** | **3-box set @ ₹249** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ₹99 | ₹198 | **₹249** |
| − Product cost (₹16/box, conservative) | −₹16 | −₹32 | −₹48 |
| − Packaging / sleeve | −₹10 | −₹10 | −₹10 |
| − Pune rider drop (Borzo ~₹50) | −₹50 | −₹50 | −₹50 |
| − Payment (UPI direct = ₹0 MDR) | ₹0 | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| **= Contribution, rider-delivered** | **₹23** | **₹106** | **₹141** |
| **= Contribution, YOU hand it over (₹0 delivery)** | **₹73** | **₹156** | **₹191** |

*Cross-checked against Finance's `unit-economics.md`: make cost ~₹9/box bottom-up, but ₹16 is used here (₹8,000 ÷ 500) to stay conservative. Rider drop ₹50 and zero-MDR UPI are Finance's verified numbers.*

### The number you must beat

> **Break-even cost-per-order on a single ₹99 box, rider-delivered: ₹23.**
> **Break-even cost-per-order on the ₹249 set, rider-delivered: ₹141.**

**₹23 is impossible.** No Meta campaign in India acquires a customer for ₹23. A single ₹99 box sent by rider is a **guaranteed loss** on every paid order.

> ## 🚨 THEREFORE: NEVER RUN ADS ON A SINGLE ₹99 BOX.
> **Every ad points at the ₹249 three-box set. Every single one.**
> That one change moves break-even from **₹23** to **₹141** — a **6x** wider margin for error. It is the entire difference between ads working and ads burning.
>
> **Second lever, free:** batch deliveries by area and **hand over as many as you can yourself**. Every self-delivered order is **₹50 more contribution** than a rider drop.

### The targets

| Result | Cost per order | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Under ₹70** | 🟢 | Working. Keep going. |
| **₹70 – ₹110** | 🟡 | Thin but alive. Improve the creative, don't scale. |
| **₹110 – ₹141** | 🟠 | Break-even. You're buying data, not profit. |
| **Over ₹141** | 🔴 | **Losing money on every order. KILL IT.** |

**Target: ₹70 per order.** That leaves about **₹71 profit** on every ₹249 sale — more if you deliver it yourself.

### What ₹3,000 realistically buys

Honest arithmetic, Pune-geo, small new account:

- CPM in Pune ≈ **₹120–₹200**. Say **₹150**.
- ₹3,000 ÷ ₹150 × 1,000 = **~20,000 impressions**
- At **1.2% CTR** → **~240 clicks** → **~₹12.50 per click**
- At **8% landing-page conversion** → **~19 orders**
- **CPO ≈ ₹155.** 🔴 **Above break-even — that version loses money.**
- At **12% conversion** → ~29 orders → **CPO ≈ ₹105.** 🟡 Thin but alive.
- **Read that again: the average outcome is roughly break-even.** Ads only clearly win if the creative is genuinely good or you hand-deliver.

> **Be clear with yourself: ₹3,000 buys roughly 20–30 orders, or about 60–90 boxes.**
> **It is a test, not a growth engine.** You are paying ₹3,000 to learn whether strangers in Pune buy this without knowing you. That answer is worth ₹3,000. The orders are a bonus.

## THE SETUP

**Platform:** Meta Ads Manager (one campaign covers Instagram + Facebook)
**Objective:** Sales / Conversions if you have a pixel firing. **No pixel? Use Traffic** to the product page, or **Messages** to Instagram DMs — DMs convert well for small Indian brands and cost nothing to set up.
**Geography:** **Pune only.** City + 15 km. **Turn OFF "include people travelling in this location."**
**Age:** 18–34
**Placements:** Automatic. Do not hand-pick on a budget this small.
**Structure:** **1 campaign → 1 ad set → 3 ads.** Let the three creatives fight inside one ad set. Do NOT split into three ad sets — ₹3,000 across three ad sets means none of them ever learns anything.

## THE 3 AUDIENCES

Run **Audience A first**. Only touch B and C if A stalls.

### A · Pune, interest-stacked — **start here**
- Pune + 15 km, age **18–32**
- Interests: specialty coffee, indie music, streetwear, stationery, graphic design, tattoo culture, film photography, thrift/vintage, sticker culture
- **Why:** closest to the person who buys a ₹99 collectible.

### B · Retargeting — **cheapest orders you will get**
- Instagram/Facebook engagers, last 90 days
- Video viewers (75%+)
- Website visitors (if pixel installed)
- **Why:** people who already looked. **Expect CPO 2–3x cheaper than cold.**
- **Build this audience on day 1 even if you don't run it yet — it's free and it needs time to fill.**

### C · Pune broad, no interests
- Pune + 15 km, **18–34**, **no interest targeting at all**
- **Why:** on small budgets Meta's algorithm often beats hand-picked interests. Worth ₹500 to find out.

## THE 3 CREATIVES TO TEST

All **9:16 vertical**. All shot on your phone. **Do not hire anyone.**

### Creative 1 — "The Strike" (video, 8 sec)
- Hand rotates the box. Close. Real light.
- **Strike. Flame.** Slow motion if your phone does it.
- Text on screen: **"Drop 01 · 500 boxes · Pune"**
- Last frame: the 3-box set + **"₹249"**
- **Testing:** does the object alone sell it?

### Creative 2 — "The Collection" (carousel or 10-sec video)
- Flat-lay of every design, top-down, one clean surface
- Text: **"Collect all [X]. When Drop 01 is gone, it's gone."**
- **Testing:** does scarcity + collectibility beat the object?

### Creative 3 — "Real Life" (UGC, 10 sec, shot badly on purpose)
- A real customer's hand pulling one off a café counter, or a real café till with your boxes on it
- Voice or text: **"₹99. Found these at a café in Koregaon Park."**
- **Testing:** does social proof beat production value? (**It usually does.**)

**Rule: every creative ends on ₹249, the 3-box set, and "Pune delivery".**

**Running total check, every single day: never let lifetime spend cross ₹3,000.**

## WHAT A GOOD RESULT LOOKS LIKE

Check these **every morning, in 5 minutes**:

| Metric | 🟢 Good | 🔴 Kill it |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost per order** | **under ₹70** | **over ₹141** |
| CTR (link) | above **1.5%** | under **0.6%** |
| CPC | under **₹8** | over **₹20** |
| 3-second hook rate | above **25%** | under **10%** |
| CPM | under **₹200** | over **₹350** |
| Orders after ₹1,000 spent | **8+** | **0–2** |

**The only metric that pays your bills is cost per order.** Likes are not sales. Reach is not sales. Saves are not sales.

## KILL CRITERIA — DECIDE NOW, NOT LATER

Write these down **before** you press publish. When money is burning you will make bad decisions.

- [ ] **₹500 spent, 0 orders** → **PAUSE.** The problem is the landing page, not the ad. Fix the page. Spend nothing more until you do.
- [ ] **₹1,000 spent, CPO above ₹200** → **kill the worst 2 creatives.** Put everything on the best one.
- [ ] **₹1,500 spent, blended CPO above ₹141** → **STOP THE WHOLE THING.** You keep the remaining ₹1,500. That is a win, not a defeat.
- [ ] **Any single creative: CTR under 0.6% after ₹300** → turn it off.
- [ ] **Any day where you feel like raising the budget "because it's working"** → **wait 48 more hours.** If it's still working, raise by ₹50/day maximum.
- [ ] **You run out of stock** → **pause immediately.** Never pay to advertise something you cannot deliver.

### The overspend lock
- Set a **Meta account spending limit of ₹3,000**. Do it on day 1. It is a hard technical stop.
- **Never** enter a card with an auto-top-up you haven't capped.
- Ads money is **₹3,000 of a ₹20,000 total.** It is 15% of everything you have.

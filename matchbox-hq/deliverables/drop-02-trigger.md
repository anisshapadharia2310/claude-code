# When can you order Drop 02?

Finance · 12 Sep 2026 · one page · read it and stick it on the wall

---

# 🚦 THE RULE

> ## Order Drop 02 only when BOTH are true:
> ## 1. **220 boxes sold.**
> ## 2. **₹15,000 in the bank.**

**Both. Not one.**

If only one is true — **wait.**

---

## Why 220 boxes

You break even at **204**.

220 is break-even plus a small cushion.

Below 204 you are still underwater. Ordering more stock while underwater is how small brands die.

220 of 300 also means the drop is genuinely selling — not just your friends being kind.

## Why ₹15,000

Drop 02 costs **₹4,300** to launch.

Paying that out of ₹15,000 leaves **₹10,700**.

That is well clear of the danger line, and it leaves the ₹5,400 reserve completely untouched.

---

# 🧱 HOW MANY TRAYS TO BUY UP FRONT

This is the single decision that makes Drop 02 cheap.

The freight is a flat **₹1,500** for one road consignment, however many trays are on it. So the more trays you buy, the cheaper each one gets.

| Trays you buy | Trays cost | + freight | **Each tray lands at** | Drops it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | ₹1,350 | ₹1,500 | **₹9.50** | Drop 01 only |
| **600** | **₹2,700** | **₹1,500** | **₹7.00** | **Drop 01 + Drop 02** |
| 900 | ₹4,050 | ₹1,500 | ₹6.17 | Drops 01, 02, 03 |
| 1,200 | ₹5,400 | ₹1,500 | ₹5.75 | Drops 01–04 |

## 👉 Buy 600 trays.

Here is why that is the right stop.

**Going from 300 to 600 saves you ₹2.50 a tray.** That is the big win. Freight stops eating you alive.

**Going from 600 to 900 only saves ₹0.83 more** — and costs another ₹1,350 of cash you have not earned yet.

**600 trays = ₹4,200 all in.** Drop 01 uses 300. Drop 02's 300 are already sitting in your room, paid for.

Buy Drop 03's trays out of Drop 02's profit, not out of your launch capital.

### Split the bag the day it arrives

Put 300 trays in a separate bag.

Write on it: **"DROP 02 — DO NOT OPEN"**.

Every tray you take out of that bag costs Drop 02 ₹7 of margin.

# Drop 02 breaks even at 73 boxes.

That is a quarter of the drop.

**Drop 01 is the tuition fee. Drop 02 is the business.**

---

## What to order, on the day the rule goes green

1. **300 printed outers only.** New artwork. Same Pune printer.
2. **Nothing else.** No new trays. No new freight. No new website.
3. **Pay the printer from cash earned, never from the reserve.**

**Budget for it: ₹3,300. Cap it at ₹4,500.**

If the printer quotes above ₹15 an outer, print 200, not 300.

---

# 🛑 THE DANGER LINE

> ## If the bank balance ever drops below ₹8,000 — stop spending. Everything. Same day.

That line sits just under **₹9,400**, which is where your balance rests after the full Drop 01 launch spend with zero sales.

If you go under ₹8,000, money is leaking somewhere you have not written down.

### The three things to do at the danger line

1. **Cancel the ads.** It is the only optional spend left. ₹3,000 back.
2. **Hand-delivery only.** No riders, no mailers. ₹78 a box instead of ₹19.
3. **Do not print a single new outer** until the balance is back above ₹15,000.

### ⚠️ The early warning

If your supplier quotes come in at the HIGH end — ₹5 a tray and ₹15 an outer — Drop 01 costs ₹12,100 and leaves you at **₹7,900**.

**That is already under the line, before you have sold anything.**

If that happens: **cut Drop 01 to 250 boxes before you place any order.** Do not print 300 outers at ₹15.

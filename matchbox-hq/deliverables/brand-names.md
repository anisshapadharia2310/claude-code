# 10 Brand Names — with real availability checks

**Read this first (30 seconds):**
- 4 of these are already taken by real businesses. I checked. Don't fall in love before you scroll.
- My TOP 3 are at the bottom.
- **You** pick the name. Not me. It's on the board as a decision.

---

## HOW I CHECKED (be honest about this)

I could **not** open WHOIS / registrar lookups or Instagram directly — this machine's network blocks them.

So every call below is **search-evidence only**:
- I searched the web for the name + "brand / company / India"
- I searched `site:instagram.com` for the handle
- I looked for an existing live site on that exact domain

**What that means for you:**
- "LOOKS TAKEN" = I found a real, live business or handle using it. Trust this. It's solid.
- "LOOKS FREE" = I found nothing using it. This is a *good signal, not proof*.
- **Before you print anything**, spend 3 minutes: type the domain into GoDaddy/Namecheap, and type the handle into the Instagram app. That's the only real test.
- Legal agent is doing the actual **trademark** search (T6). Domain free ≠ trademark free.

---

## ⚠️ THE TOBACCO RULE — read before you pick

India's COTPA law + ad rules are brutal about anything that looks like tobacco advertising. Surrogate advertising gets brands into real trouble.

**So:**
- ❌ No name that sounds like a cigarette: no "Gold", "Kings", "Classic", "Navy Cut", "Charminar", "Regal", "Filter", "Smoke", "Drag", "Puff", "Ash".
- ❌ Never say the product is "for smokers" — not on the box, not in a caption, not to a café owner.
- ✅ The brand is about **fire, art, collecting**. Candles, incense, diyas, chai, birthday cakes, camping, blackouts, sparklers, photography.
- I killed some genuinely good-sounding options (things like "Ash Club") for exactly this reason.
- If a name makes a stranger think *cigarettes* in under 2 seconds — it's a legal liability, not a brand.

# THE 10

## 1. KAADEPETI — काडेपेटी
- **Means:** Literally "matchbox" in Marathi. The word every Pune household already uses.
- **Why it works:** It IS the product, in the local language. Instantly Pune. Zero translation needed for aaji, feels like a flex for Gen Z. Very ownable — nobody's used it as a brand.
- **Vibe:** Local · Honest · Warm
- **Tagline:** *"आपली काडेपेटी."* (Your matchbox.) — or English: "The matchbox, but make it yours."
- **Availability:** 🟢 **LOOKS FREE** — searched "kaadepeti / kadepeti / kaddipetti brand": only hits are a Marathi children's science book (*Kadepeti aani Itar Vidnyan Khelani*, Arvind Gupta). No company, no brand. `kaadepeti.com` did not resolve to any site. `site:instagram.com` search returned no @kaadepeti. **Confirm on a registrar + the IG app yourself.**
- **Risk:** It's a common noun — trademark may be limited. Ask Legal (T6).

## 2. KAADI — काडी
- **Means:** "Stick" in Marathi/Hindi. Short, blunt, five letters.
- **Why it works:** Shortest possible. Looks incredible on a tiny box. One word you can own visually — a single black stick as the logo.
- **Vibe:** Sharp · Minimal · Desi
- **Tagline:** "One kaadi. One moment."
- **Availability:** 🟡 **COULD NOT CHECK properly / caution** — no matching Indian product brand found, but there's a LinkedIn company page called "Kaadi" (unclear what it does), and a big phonetic clash: **Khaadi**, a major Pakistani fashion retailer with UK/Middle East stores. Same sound. That could be a trademark headache and will pollute your search results.
- **Risk:** Medium. Nice name, noisy neighbourhood.

## 3. UJED — उजेड
- **Means:** "Light / glow" in Marathi. Not "lamp", not "lighting" — the *light itself*, as in "there's light in the room".
- **Why it works:** Poetic without being soft. Perfect for a brand about the 3 seconds after you strike. Marathi speakers get a little jolt of recognition; everyone else just sees a cool 4-letter word.
- **Vibe:** Poetic · Marathi · Quiet
- **Tagline:** "थोडा उजेड." (A little light.)
- **Availability:** 🟡 **PARTLY TAKEN / CHECK** — no Indian brand uses it. BUT Universidad Juárez del Estado de Durango (Mexico) owns **@ujed_oficial** on IG and dominates all "UJED" search results. Plain @ujed is unknown to me. `ujed.in` showed no live site in search.
- **Risk:** You'd be fighting a Mexican university for Google. `ujed.in` + a handle like `@ujed.co` fixes most of it.

## 4. DECCAN MATCHWORKS
- **Means:** Deccan = the plateau Pune sits on, and Deccan Gymkhana / Deccan Queen. "Matchworks" = the old factory naming style (Sivakasi factories are literally called "X Match Works").
- **Why it works:** Instant heritage. Sounds like a 1940s factory that a design kid resurrected — which is exactly the collectible story. Gives you unlimited "archive series" box designs.
- **Vibe:** Heritage · Printed · Pune
- **Tagline:** "Struck in Pune. Since now."
- **Availability:** 🟢 **LOOKS FREE** — searched "Deccan + match works / matchworks": real Match Works companies exist (Manickam, Janaki, G.D. — all Tamil Nadu, generic naming) and a "Deccan Group of Companies" (doors/windows, different category). Nothing named Deccan Matchworks. Long domain = likely free.
- **Risk:** Two words, long handle. "Deccan" is used a lot in Pune generally.

## 5. PINKHEAD
- **Means:** Literally the product. Pink head, black stick.
- **Why it works:** Blunt English, fully Gen Z, sounds like a sticker brand. The name explains the photo. Zero explanation needed on a marketplace listing.
- **Vibe:** Blunt · Poppy · Gen Z
- **Tagline:** "Pink head. Black stick. No notes."
- **Availability:** 🟡 **LOOKS FREE-ISH / COULD NOT CHECK** — no company found using Pinkhead as a brand. Only generic "pink head" product listings (Etsy headbands, fishing lures) and an Italian wallpaper *pattern* called Pinkhead. No IG result surfaced.
- **Risk:** Descriptive names are weak trademarks. And it boxes you in if you ever make a non-pink box. Fix: **@pinkhead.co** with a strong logo does the work.

## 6. SMALL FIRES
- **Means:** What you're actually selling — tiny, repeatable fires.
- **Why it works:** Reads like a poetry zine or an indie candle brand. The art/design crowd in Koregaon Park will love it.
- **Vibe:** Artsy · Soft · Literary
- **Tagline:** "Start a small fire."
- **Availability:** 🔴 **LOOKS TAKEN** — **@smallfires exists on Instagram** (found via site:instagram.com). Also **Various Small Fires** is a well-known LA contemporary art gallery — which is the exact crowd you're targeting, so you'd look derivative.
- **Verdict:** Skip.

## 7. TEELI — तीली
- **Means:** "Matchstick" in Hindi.
- **Why it works:** Soft, feminine sound for a fire product. Nice contrast.
- **Vibe:** Soft · Hindi · Cute
- **Tagline:** "Ek teeli, bas."
- **Availability:** 🔴 **LOOKS TAKEN** — **teeli.com is a live site** (Riensch & Held, a German tea-filter/household manufacturer). On IG, **@teelipk** is a Pakistani comedy channel with ~169K followers dominating the name. Also teeli.net and Teeli Jewels exist.
- **Verdict:** Skip. Crowded.

## 8. KADAK
- **Means:** Pune-Marathi for strong/sharp — kadak chai, kadak awaaz. Highest local slang value on this list.
- **Why it works:** A Punekar smiles when they read it. Perfect chai-adjacent positioning (and chai is a legal, safe context — unlike the other thing).
- **Vibe:** Strong · Slangy · Local
- **Tagline:** "Kadak, first strike."
- **Availability:** 🔴 **LOOKS TAKEN** — **kadak.com is live** (KADAK Products Ltd). Also: Kadak Family Tea Pvt Ltd (Kanpur, CIN registered), a Mumbai craft beer called Kadak, kadakmerch.com, an Amazon "Kadak Collection" store, IG **@kadaklab** and **@kadak.original**.
- **Verdict:** Skip. Beautiful word, hopelessly crowded.

## 9. PHULJHADI — फुलझडी
- **Means:** Sparkler. Diwali in one word.
- **Why it works:** Joy, festival, tiny controlled fire, giftable.
- **Vibe:** Festive · Happy · Nostalgic
- **Tagline:** "Chhoti si phuljhadi."
- **Availability:** 🔴 **LOOKS TAKEN** — **phuljhadi.com is a live Shopify store** (jewellery + sunglasses brand, founded 2018 by Prachi Khanduja, Delhi), with IG, LinkedIn and a Facebook page.
- **Verdict:** Skip.

## 10. BURN BOOK
- **Means:** Mean Girls reference. Gen Z shorthand for "a small object full of chaos you keep".
- **Why it works:** The *collectible* angle is baked into the name — a book you collect. Extremely memeable.
- **Vibe:** Meme · Chaotic · Pink
- **Tagline:** "Keep the book. Strike the page."
- **Availability:** 🟡 **COULD NOT CHECK / RISKY** — a US trademark BURNBOOK (serial 85347501, Zane Jacobs) exists but is **ABANDONED**. No live brand found using it for physical goods. But: it's a Paramount film reference, it's a widely-used phrase, and it's near-impossible to trademark cleanly.
- **Risk:** Fun, legally soft. Better as a **box design name** than the company name. (See box-concepts.md.)

# 🏆 MY TOP 3

### A. KAADEPETI
The most ownable and the most *Pune*. It's the product's real name in the city's real language — which is exactly the joke a collectible box wants. Cleanest availability signal on the whole list. A tourist won't get it; that's the point. Instagram bio writes itself: *"काडेपेटी. Pink head, black stick. Made to keep."*

### B. DECCAN MATCHWORKS
The best *story*. "Matchworks" is a real category word, so it makes you look like a manufacturer with history instead of a Canva side project — which is worth a lot at ₹99. It also unlocks endless box series (Deccan Matchworks No. 01, No. 02…) which drives collecting. Downside: long.

### C. PINKHEAD
The most *scroll-stopping*. It's what people will literally call you anyway ("that pink matchstick brand"). Best for marketplaces and for someone finding you via a Reel. Weakest trademark, strongest recall.

**If you made me pick: KAADEPETI**, with `@kaadepeti` and `kaadepeti.in`, and "Pinkhead" kept alive as the name of Drop 01's flagship box. Local word + global-looking product = the thing people photograph.

### Before you lock anything (10 min of your life)
1. Type the domain into any registrar. `.in` first — cheaper and more credible for Pune-only shipping.
2. Type the handle into the Instagram app. Also grab it on the app even if you don't use it.
3. Ping Legal agent (T6) to run the real trademark search on your pick.
4. Then, and only then, put the name on a box.

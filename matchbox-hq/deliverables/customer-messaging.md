## 🚫 THE TWO RULES THAT NEVER BEND

### 1. Never sell this as a smoking product.
Not in a caption, not in a DM, not as a joke, not in reply to a customer who says it first.

| ❌ Never say | ✅ Say instead |
|---|---|
| "for your smoke breaks" | "for candles, incense, diyas, your stove" |
| "rolling / lighting up / stoner" | "collectible", "keeps on your shelf" |
| "better than a lighter" | "nicer than a lighter" |
| any tobacco, weed, vape or 420 reference | the object, the art, the design |

If a customer jokes about it: **do not joke back in writing.** Reply about the design. Screenshots travel.

### 2. Never promise a delivery date you do not control.
**Matches are UN 1944, Class 4.1 flammable solids. No courier will carry them.**
Shiprocket, Blue Dart and India Post all refuse. **Pune hand-delivery is the only channel.**
**You may never give a non-Pune person a date. Not "soon". Not "a few weeks". A date is a promise.**

## 5 · THE DELAY APOLOGY (send it BEFORE they ask, not after)

**The rule: the moment you know you're late, they know you're late.** A delay you announce is a small problem. A delay they discover is a refund.

```
[NAME] — straight up, I'm running late on your box.

What happened: [ONE SENTENCE. THE REAL REASON. NO EXCUSES.]
New date: [NEW DATE]. That's the date I'm confident about, not the hopeful one.

Two options, your call:
1. Wait — I'll add a second box free for the trouble.
2. Full refund right now, back to the same UPI, today. No hard feelings.

Just tell me 1 or 2 🙏
Sorry. This is on me.
— [YOUR NAME]
```

**If it slips a second time — different message, and the refund goes first:**

```
[NAME], I've missed it again and that's not okay.

I'm refunding you now — ₹[AMOUNT] back to [UPI], you'll see it within the hour.
You keep the box when it lands, free. You paid on faith and I didn't hold up my end.

If you'd rather I just didn't send it, say so and that's fine too.
```

**Never:** "due to unforeseen circumstances", "our logistics partner", "we". You are one person in Pune. Say "I".

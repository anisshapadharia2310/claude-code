*Costs you: two evenings and a friend*

- Reject anything with a crushed corner. Keep a reject count - that is your real breakage rate.

## Where a delay kills the drop date

Ranked by how likely it is to actually happen to you.

### 1. The brand name is not locked by Mon 28 Sep — **MOST LIKELY KILLER**
Decision D-002 is still open. The printer cannot start without final artwork, and artwork cannot be final without a name.
**Every day the name slips, 24 Oct slips.** This is not a printing problem, it is a decision problem.
→ **Hard deadline: name decided by Fri 25 Sep.**

### 2. Sample courier runs long
Surface transport of matches is slow and untracked. 8 days budgeted, 12 happens.
→ Get the docket number on day one. Chase on day 6.
→ **Buffer: none. This one eats straight into the schedule.**

### 3. Every vendor's MOQ turns out to be 5,000+
Then the whole plan changes and you lose 2 weeks finding someone smaller.
→ This is why you ask MOQ in the first 60 seconds on Mon 14 Sep, not after samples arrive.

### 4. The tray dimensions vary
If your 10 measured trays differ by more than 1mm, the printer's die will fit some sleeves and not others. You find this out **after** printing 500. Disaster.
→ Measure 10 trays from the samples. Send a real tray to the printer. Both of these exist to stop exactly this.

### 5. Printer proof rejected
One round: +4 days. Two rounds: +8 days and you miss 24 Oct.
→ Get the proof right the first time. Check text size, colour, fold line, legal panel. Have Brand and Legal both look at it before you approve.

### 6. Paying the tray advance on a Friday evening
Sivakasi runs on advances and nobody starts a run at 6pm Friday. A Friday payment is really a Monday start.
→ **Pay on a Monday morning.** That is why money out #2 is on Mon 28 Sep.

## If you need boxes faster than 24 Oct

Only one lever actually works: **buy trays from a vendor who already has plain stock on the shelf.**
Ask on every call: *"Do you have plain filled boxes in stock right now, or do you make to order?"*
In-stock trays cut 2-3 weeks off the schedule and move the date to roughly **6 October**.

Everything else - paying more, shouting, splitting the order - does not move a Sivakasi production line.

# Laws To Remember

Pin this. One line each. Full detail in `drop-01-law-map.md`.

1. **Pune only.** One box outside Maharashtra = compulsory GST + monthly filings forever.
2. **Never sell above the printed MRP.** Below is always fine. Above is an offence.
3. **Count the sticks before you print the count.** Box says 50, holds 40 = short measure.
4. **No GST wording on any invoice.** You're unregistered — "GST not applicable", nothing else.
5. **One unbroken invoice number series.** Every sale, including friends and cash.
6. **The claimed counter must be the real number.** Fake scarcity is a listed dark pattern.
7. **Grievance officer is you** — name, phone, email in the site footer. Reply in 48 hours.
8. **Never refuse a refund** on a damaged, wrong or late box. No cancellation fees.
9. **No cigarettes, ashtrays or "light up" in any post.** Light a candle instead.
10. **Every seeded box needs #Ad or #Collab** in the first two lines. A free box counts as payment.
11. **Claim nothing you can't prove** — not eco-friendly, not BIS, not child-safe, not No.1.
12. **Legal Metrology packer registration: ₹500, before boxes go on public sale.** Only real cost.

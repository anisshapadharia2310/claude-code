## 🛑 The danger line

> **If the bank balance ever drops below ₹8,000, stop spending. Everything. Immediately.**

This moved down from ₹11,000. Here is why, honestly: the boxes cost more, so the balance sits lower after launch. At the mid cost it sits at **₹9,400** before a single sale. ₹8,000 is the floor under that.

If it goes under ₹8,000, money is leaking somewhere you have not counted.

Three things to do at the danger line:

1. **Cancel the ad budget.** It is the only optional spend left. That is ₹3,000 back.
2. **Sell by hand only.** No rider, no mailers. ₹78 a box instead of ₹18.70.
3. **Do not print a single new outer** until the balance is back above ₹15,000.

⚠️ If your supplier quotes land at the HIGH end (₹5 tray, ₹15 outer), the balance after launch is ₹7,900 — already under the line. **In that case cut Drop 01 to 250 boxes before you order anything.**

## The three things that would break this plan

1. **The printer will not do 300 outers, or quotes above ₹15.** This is now your biggest single cost. Get three Pune quotes before you commit. Above ₹15 an outer, the whole plan needs resizing.
2. **Couriers refuse to carry matches.** They officially do — UN 1944, Class 4.1. Keep Drop 01 inside Pune and hand-delivered.
3. **You use Drop 02's trays in Drop 01.** 300 of the 600 trays are not yours to touch. Put them in a separate bag and label it "DROP 02 — DO NOT OPEN". Every one you use in Drop 01 costs Drop 02 ₹7 of margin. Cap the seeding giveaway at 20 boxes.

# Sample plan - what to order first

**Written 12 Sep 2026 - Production & Ops**

## The order, in one box

> **50 sample boxes from each of 3 match vendors** = 150 boxes
> **Plus 1 printed outer prototype** from a Pune printer
> **Budget: ₹1,500 for the matches + ₹800 for the prototype = ₹2,300 max**

Nothing else gets bought until these land and score.

## Why 50 and not 100

Your own call script says 50-100. Take the low end.

- 50 boxes is enough to strike 30 and still have 20 intact to show people.
- 50 x 3 vendors = 150 boxes for the same money as 100 from one vendor.
- **Three opinions beat one big sample.** You are choosing a vendor, not testing a product.

Go to 100 only if a vendor says 50 is too fiddly to pack - some will.

## What a sample should cost

| | ₹ |
|---|---|
| 50 boxes of goods (at ₹4-5) | 200 - 250 |
| Courier / bus parcel to Pune | 150 - 300 |
| **Realistic per vendor** | **₹350 - 550** |
| **Your cap per vendor** | **₹800** |

### The rules

- **Offer to pay.** Your script already does this. It gets you taken seriously.
- **Over ₹800 for 50 boxes + courier is a no.** Say "that's more than I can do for samples" and move on. A vendor who overcharges for samples will overcharge on the order.
- **Some will send free.** Exporters often do. Let them offer - never ask first.
- **Matches are hazardous goods.** Air courier will refuse them. Ask: *"How do you normally send to Maharashtra?"* Expect surface transport or bus parcel. Get the transporter's name and the docket number.
- **Pay by UPI and screenshot it.** No cash, no "I'll pay on delivery".

## Also order: ONE printed outer prototype

Before you spend ₹5,500 on 500 sleeves, spend ₹300-800 to hold one.

- **Unbox (Parvati Industrial Estate)** - their site claims they can make a single custom box on a sample machine. Ask them to confirm.
- **Printo Hinjewadi** - cheaper, cruder: print your artwork flat on card for ₹50-100 and fold it around a tray yourself. Not a real box, but it shows you the design at actual size.

**Wait until at least one set of match samples lands**, so the printer has a real tray to measure. Do not guess the dimensions.

## What to inspect when the box arrives

Open it on a table with good light. Do this properly once - it saves you ₹5,000 later.

### 1. HEAD COLOUR
- Is it actually black, or dark brown pretending to be black?
- Are all 40 heads the same colour, or does the box have a mixed bag?
- Photograph it next to a white sheet. That is what your Instagram will look like.

### 2. STRIKE RELIABILITY - **this is the gate**
- Take 3 boxes. Strike **10 matches from each** = 30 strikes.
- Count: lit first try / lit second try / head broke off / did not light.
- **Under 9 out of 10 first-try = automatic fail, no matter how good it looks.**
- Also check the strip: does it survive 40 strikes, or is it bald by number 20?

### 3. THE STICK
- Carbonised (black/dark, burns clean) or plain wood?
- Does it burn steadily for 8+ seconds without curling or dropping embers?
- Any smell of damp? Damp matches arriving in September in Pune = a supplier with a storage problem.
- Bend one. It should snap, not bend.

### 4. FIT - **the one everyone forgets**
- Measure the tray with a ruler: length x width x height in mm.
- **Do all 50 trays measure the same?** Measure 10 of them. Sivakasi trays vary.
- Does the tray slide into a sleeve smoothly - not loose, not jammed?
- Count sticks in 5 boxes. Is it 40 every time, or 36-44?
- **Variation here is the single biggest risk to the whole model.** If trays vary by 1mm, your printed sleeves will fit some and not others, and you will have paid for 500 of them.

### 5. THE BOX ITSELF
- Is the plain tray clean enough to be seen, or does it need the sleeve to cover it completely?
- Any glue smears, crushed corners, torn edges?
- How many of the 50 arrived damaged? That is your breakage rate at scale.

### 6. PRINT QUALITY (the outer prototype)
- Do your colours match the screen, or has everything gone muddy?
- Is the smallest legal text still readable? (Legal & Setup set a 1mm minimum.)
- Does matte lamination scuff when you rub it with your thumb?
- Does the fold crack along the crease?

## Scoring sheet

Copy this per vendor. **Score each out of 5. 30 points total.**

| # | What | Score /5 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Head colour - properly black, consistent | | |
| 2 | **Strike reliability (out of 30 strikes)** | | **GATE** |
| 3 | Stick - carbonised, clean burn, no damp | | |
| 4 | Fit + consistency (10 trays measured) | | |
| 5 | Box condition on arrival | | |
| 6 | How they dealt with you (speed, WhatsApp photos, straight answers) | | |
| | **TOTAL** | **/30** | |

### How to read it

- **22+** = order from them.
- **18-21** = usable, but negotiate. Say exactly what was wrong.
- **Under 18** = no.
- **Criterion 2 under 27/30 strikes = automatic no**, even at 29 total points. A matchbox that does not light is not a product.

### Tiebreaker
If two vendors score within 2 points, pick the one who **answered faster and sent WhatsApp photos**. You will be dealing with them at 11pm before a drop. Responsiveness compounds.

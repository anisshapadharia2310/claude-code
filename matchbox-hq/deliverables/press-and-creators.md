# PRESS & CREATORS — Pune

Sales & PR · 12 Sep 2026 · Budget for this: **₹0 cash + 20 free boxes (≈₹360 of stock)**

> ⚠️ **I have not invented a single journalist, editor, handle, phone number or email address.**
> Everything below is **types of outlet**, **how to find the real one**, and **what to send**.
> Where I verified a real outlet exists, the URL is there. **Open it yourself before you pitch.**

---

## 🔥 WHY THIS IS A STORY (lead with this, not the brand name)

You have four angles. **Use one per pitch. Never all four.**

| # | The angle | Who it's for | The hook line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **₹20,000 total budget.** A whole brand, start to finish, for the price of a phone. | Startup / business / college press | "The entire company cost less than a phone." |
| 2 | **Reviving Sivakasi matchbox art.** India's matchbox labels were a real miniature-art movement, now mostly gone. | Culture, design, art pages | "India's smallest art form, brought back at ₹99." |
| 3 | **The courier that says no.** Matches are **UN 1944, Class 4.1 flammable solids** — no courier will carry them. So the brand delivers every box by hand, in Pune. | City pages, local news, business | "A brand that legally cannot be shipped — so it's delivered by hand." |
| 4 | **Made in Pune, sold in Pune.** A city brand you can only get in this city. | City culture, café pages | "You can only buy it here." |

**Angle 3 is your strongest and nobody expects it.** A constraint is a better story than a product.

**Angle 2 is real, not marketing** — matchbox labels are a documented Indian graphic-art tradition:
- [The National — what Indian matchboxes say about the country's art history](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/art/2021/09/14/what-matchboxes-from-india-say-about-the-countrys-art-history/)
- [Homegrown — Maachis.art is reviving Indian matchbox art](https://homegrown.co.in/homegrown-supply/maachis-is-turning-vintage-matchbox-labels-into-a-living-archive)
- [Tiny Canvases, Big Stories: rise, fall and revival of matchbox art in India](https://blog.alliance.edu.in/tiny-canvases-big-stories-the-rise-fall-and-revival-of-matchbox-art-in-india/)
- [Twos Studio — Matchboxes of India: the forgotten pop art](https://twosstudio.com/articles/matchboxes-of-india-the-forgotten-pop-art)

**Read one of these before any press call.** If a journalist asks "why matchboxes?" and you say "they look nice", the story dies. If you say "Sivakasi labels were a miniature art movement and almost nobody prints new ones", you get a piece.

🚫 **Never in any pitch:** anything about smoking, tobacco, vaping or 420. It kills the story and the brand.

## 📰 PART 1 — WHICH KINDS OF OUTLETS

Ranked by how likely they are to actually run you. **Start at the top.**

### Tier A — will genuinely cover a small Pune brand

| Kind of outlet | Why they'd run it | How to find the real one |
|---|---|---|
| **City lifestyle guides with a Pune section** | They exist to find new local things. Verified: [LBB Pune](https://lbb.in/pune/), [CityShor Pune](https://www.cityshor.com/pune/) | Their site has a "submit"/"partner" or contact page. **Check whether it's editorial or paid** — LBB's model includes paid brand features ([Forbes India on LBB](https://www.forbesindia.com/article/self-made-women-2020/how-suchita-salwan-took-lbb-from-blog-to-brand-marketplace/58101/1)). **Do not pay. You have ₹20,000.** |
| **Pune city Instagram pages** (food, café, "things to do in Pune") | Constant appetite for new local content, post daily | IG search: `pune food`, `pune cafe`, `things to do pune`, `punediaries`-style tags. Check the last 9 posts are real Pune places, not reposts. |
| **College / campus publications** | Your exact buyer, and they reply fast. Verified example: [Campus Times Pune](https://www.campustimespune.com/) | Most Pune colleges have a media/culture cell on IG. Also FC Road / Deccan campus mags. |
| **Design & illustration accounts** (Indian, not Pune-only) | The Sivakasi angle is their whole feed | IG/Behance search: Indian packaging design, matchbox label, Indian type design, riso/print accounts |
| **Local newsletters & community groups** | Small list, very high trust | Pune co-working spaces, running/cycling clubs, book clubs, flea-market organisers — most send a weekly mail |

### Tier B — worth trying, slower

| Kind of outlet | Note |
|---|---|
| **Pune city newspapers / tabloids** | Verified to exist: [Pune Times Mirror / Civic Mirror](https://civicmirror.in/). City desks cover local enterprise. Find the **city desk email on their own contact page** — never guess an address. |
| **National culture/youth publications with an Indian-design beat** | e.g. [Homegrown](https://homegrown.co.in/) covers Indian design and craft revival. Big reach, low Pune conversion. Pitch angle 2 only. |
| **Pune society / city magazines** | Verified: [Incredible Pune](https://www.incrediblepune.com/). Also [Go Pune](https://www.cityshor.com/pune/know-about-everything-in-pune-with-go-pune/), a Pune city guide magazine. |
| **Startup & small-business press** | Angle 1 (the ₹20,000). Low priority — their readers aren't buyers. |
| **Podcasts: Pune, D2C, or design** | Long lead time, but one episode = a long-lived asset |

### Tier C — skip at launch
National newspapers · TV · paid PR agencies · anything asking for money · anything that can't send a Pune reader to your DMs.

**The test for every outlet: can its readers actually buy this?** If they're not in Pune, they cannot. **A Pune page with 4,000 followers beats a national page with 400,000.**

## ✉️ PART 2 — THE 90-WORD PITCH EMAIL

Subject line — pick one:
- `Pune brand that legally can't be couriered`
- `A ₹20,000 matchbox brand made in Pune`
- `Reviving Sivakasi matchbox art — Pune, 300 boxes`

**Body (90 words — do not make it longer):**

> Hi [NAME FROM THEIR OWN SITE],
>
> I'm [YOUR NAME], from Pune. I make collectible matchboxes — printed the way Sivakasi labels used to be, back when matchbox art was a real Indian art form.
>
> Two things that might be a story:
>
> The whole brand cost **₹20,000** — design, stock, everything.
>
> And matches are classed as flammable goods, so no courier in India will carry them. Every box is **hand-delivered in Pune**, by me.
>
> First drop is **300 boxes, ₹99 each**. Happy to send you one.
>
> [YOUR NAME] · [PHONE] · [INSTAGRAM]

**Rules:**
- **Attach nothing.** One photo pasted inline, maximum.
- **No press kit. No PDF. No "media enquiry".**
- Name a real person from their site. If you can't find one, write `Hi team —`.
- **Send Tuesday–Thursday, 10–11am.**
- **One follow-up only, 5 days later, 2 lines.** Then stop forever.

**The follow-up:**

> Hi [NAME] — bumping this once in case it got buried. Drop 01 opens [DATE], happy to send a box either way. Thanks for reading. — [YOUR NAME]

## 💬 PART 3 — THE 40-WORD DM (creators)

**40 words. Count them. Longer = ignored.**

```
Hi [NAME] — I make collectible matchboxes in Pune, printed like old
Sivakasi labels. First run is 300. Can I send you one? No post needed,
no strings — I just want it in your hands 🙏
```

**Why "no post needed" works:** it removes the ask, so they reply. Then they usually post anyway. Ask for a post up front and your reply rate halves.

**Variants — change one line only:**

| Creator type | Swap the middle line to |
|---|---|
| Café / food reviewer | "…they're going on counters at a few Pune cafés this month. Want one before they do?" |
| Designer / illustrator | "…every box is a new printed design, like the old Sivakasi labels. Want one?" |
| College page | "…₹99, made in Pune, first run of 300. Want one for the page?" |
| Photographer | "…they photograph stupidly well. Want one to shoot?" |

**After they say yes:**

```
Amazing. Where in Pune are you? I'll drop it myself this week.
If you do end up posting, tag [@HANDLE] — that's genuinely the only
thing that helps me. No pressure either way 🙏
```

## 🎯 PART 4 — CREATOR TIERING & WHAT TO OFFER

> 🔴 **The free-box budget is 20 boxes. TOTAL. For everything.**
> Finance capped it (`break-even.md`) because Drop 01 is now **300 boxes, not 500**, and each one costs **₹18 landed**, not ₹9. 20 free boxes = **₹360 of stock** = your entire marketing spend.
> ⚠️ This **supersedes** `seeding-plan.md`, which was written for a 500-box run and budgeted 60 boxes. **60 boxes is now 20% of the whole drop. Do not do it.**

### The 20 free boxes — spend them like this

| Who | Boxes |
|---|---|
| **Micro creators** (1 each) | **12** |
| **Mid creators** (1 each — not a set) | **3** |
| **Press / journalists** | **2** |
| **Café counter samples** (only where the owner actually engaged) | **3** |
| **TOTAL** | **20** |

⚠️ `cafe-pitch.md` says leave a free sample at every visit. **At 40 visits that is 40 boxes — twice the whole free budget.** New rule: **a sample box only goes to a venue that said yes or asked you to come back.** Everyone else gets the leave-behind sheet and nothing else.

| Tier | Size | Boxes | What you offer | What you ask for | Realistic result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MICRO** | **1k–10k** | **12** | 1 box, hand-delivered, + their name on the Drop 02 thank-you list | A story with a tag. Nothing more. | **~6 will post.** Best value in the whole plan. |
| **MID** | **10k–50k** | **3** | 1 box + **first pick of Drop 02 designs** + a discount code with their name on it + **2 free boxes from Drop 02** when it lands | One feed post OR one reel, their words, their timing | **~1–2 will post.** Treat each as a real conversation, not a DM blast. |
| **MACRO** | **50k+** | **0** | **Nothing. Do not contact them.** | — | They will ask for money you don't have. |

**Why the mid tier gets paid in Drop 02 stock:** a Drop 02 box costs you **₹11, not ₹18** — the trays are already bought. **Promising future boxes is the cheapest currency you own.** Use it instead of cash, every time.

### The rules

- **Never pay cash.** ₹3,000 of ad money is locked (`pune-ads-plan.md`) and this is not what it's for.
- **Never send a box before they reply.** A box sent cold is ₹20 in a drawer.
- **Hand-deliver every creator box yourself.** You meet them, they remember you, they post. Couriered = forgotten.
- **A discount code is free to you and feels big to them.** Use it instead of money, always.
- **12 micro > 1 mid > 0 macro.** Twelve Pune people holding your box beats one big account.
- **Follow up once. Never twice.** Chasing a creator costs you the relationship for Drop 02.

### How to find them without inventing anyone

1. Instagram search: `pune cafe`, `pune food`, `pune design`, `punestagram`, `things to do in pune`
2. Open the **location tag** of 5 independent Koregaon Park cafés → scroll who posts there → follow the real accounts
3. Check the **tagged photos** of any Pune café you like
4. Pune flea markets and design fairs — the stallholders' accounts are exactly your tier
5. Ask every customer: **"who in Pune posts about stuff like this?"**

**Vet each one in 60 seconds:**

- [ ] Posted in the last **7 days**
- [ ] Comments are real sentences, not 🔥🔥 from bot accounts
- [ ] They post about **objects and places**, not just their own face
- [ ] **Pune, actually** — check their tags and stories, not their bio
- [ ] They've posted a small brand before, unpaid

## 📅 PART 5 — THE 4-WEEK RUN

| Week | Press | Creators | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Build the list: **10 real outlets** with a real contact page. No pitches yet. | Build a list of **40 real Pune accounts**. Follow them. Comment on 3 posts each. | 10 outlets + 40 accounts listed |
| **2** | Send **10 pitches.** Tues–Thurs, 10am. | Send **20 DMs.** 5 a day. | **3 replies** · **6 creator yeses** |
| **3** | Follow up once. Send **10 more** to a different outlet type. | Hand-deliver **12 micro boxes**. Take a photo with every one. | **1 piece confirmed** · **12 boxes out** |
| **4** | Send any published link to every other outlet: "this ran, want your own angle?" | Deliver **3 mid boxes** + **2 press boxes**. Repost every post within 1 hour. | **2 pieces live** · **7 posts live** |

**Expected from the whole run:** 20 pitches → **1–2 pieces**. 40 DMs → **4–7 posts**. That is a normal, good outcome. (Rates are in `sales-pipeline.md`.)

## 📦 THE PRESS BOX — what you hand a journalist or a mid-tier creator

- [ ] **1 box.** One. Not a set — the free budget is 20 boxes total.
- [ ] **A handwritten note.** Their name. One line. This is the whole trick.
- [ ] **One A5 sheet**, five lines only:
  - What it is · **₹99** · Pune, hand-delivered
  - Drop 01 = **300 boxes**
  - **Whole brand built on ₹20,000**
  - **UN 1944 Class 4.1** — couriers refuse matches, so it's hand-delivered
  - Your name · phone · Instagram
- [ ] **3 photos on a link** (Drive/Dropbox): box in a hand · flat-lay of all designs · box on a café counter. **Named files. No watermarks.**

**Never send:** a press release, a deck, a PDF brochure, a "media kit", or a box to someone who hasn't replied.

## ⚠️ FIVE WAYS THIS GOES WRONG

1. **Pitching before you have stock.** A piece runs, people click, there's nothing to buy. **Pitch only after the 60-box gate is passed and the order is placed.**
2. **Paying for a "feature".** Several city guides sell placements. **You cannot afford it and it does not convert.**
3. **Mentioning smoking.** Instant kill. See `customer-messaging.md`.
4. **Promising a non-Pune journalist a box.** You cannot post it. Offer it **when they're next in Pune**, or send photos.
5. **Inventing a number to sound bigger.** Pune café owners and Pune journalists talk to each other. **Say the real pre-order number, even when it's 12.**

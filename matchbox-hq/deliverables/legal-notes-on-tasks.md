### G1 / P2 — Printing the box (Brand + Production)

**Nothing goes to a printer until the back-face legal block is final and correct** —
the 8 mandatory declarations, minimum 1.0 mm type (use 5–6pt), real postal address with
PIN, and the factory's exact legal name in writing. Reprinting 500 outers is not free,
and the offence is committed at the printing, not at the sale.

**Second line for the 3-box set:** the outer sleeve is a *multi-piece package* and needs
its own full declarations — MRP ₹279, net quantity "3 boxes", marketer and manufacturer.
The inner boxes keep their own labels; don't strip them.

### W1 — Website waitlist and pre-order flow (Website)

**Your own site makes you an "inventory e-commerce entity" under the Consumer Protection
(E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** — the footer needs legal name, full address, customer care
contact and a named **Grievance Officer** (that's the founder), plus a published
returns/refund policy and the total price shown as one figure.

**The claimed counter must always show the true number** — you already built it to be
typed in by hand, and that is now a legal reason, not just an honest one. False urgency
is #1 on the CCPA's dark-patterns list. **Never auto-increment it.**

**And on the pre-order page, above the pay button:** "This is a pre-order, ships from
[DATE], Pune hand-delivery only, full refund if we can't deliver to you."

### G2 — Instagram content (Brand)

**No cigarettes, ashtrays, rolling papers, joints or anyone smoking — ever, in any frame.**
Light a candle or incense in the demo shot. Meta's ad policy bans tobacco paraphernalia
and a reviewer won't read your explanation; repeat rejections restrict the ad account.

**Every gifted or seeded post needs #Ad, #Sponsored or #Collab in the first two lines of
the caption** (verbal disclosure in the first 10 seconds for video). A free box is a
material connection under ASCI, and brand and creator are jointly liable.

### T15 — Café wholesale pitch (Sales)

**A carton of boxes for a café is a "wholesale package"** — write the brand name and
address, what's inside, and the number of retail boxes on the carton. Marker pen is fine.

**Give them a plain numbered invoice with no GST on it** — you're unregistered, so you
cannot issue a tax invoice or a bill of supply, and you must not write "GST" anywhere.
Also: **the café can sell at or below ₹99, never above it.** Say that out loud at the pitch.

### P1 — The Sivakasi vendor calls (Production)

**Your enquiry asks for pink and black match heads and dyed black sticks — ask every
vendor, in writing: "Is this a safety match conforming to IS 2653, or a colour/star
match?"** Indian law treats colour matches very differently from safety matches, and
the answer decides whether any of the rest of our plan holds. Screenshot the reply.

**In the same message, collect:** their **GSTIN**, their **BIS licence number**, and the
**exact legal name and address** they want printed as "Manufactured by" on your box.
You cannot finalise artwork without that last one.

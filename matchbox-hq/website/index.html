Questions

FAQ

What is the difference between the waitlist and the pre-order?

The waitlist is always open, costs nothing, and is how we tell you about every drop. The pre-order only exists while a drop window is open, and that is the one where you claim an actual box. You can be on the waitlist and pre-order too.

Am I paying now?

No. Right now you are joining the waitlist. Nothing is charged.

How much is a box?

₹99 for one box. The three-box set is ₹249, which is the cheapest way to own all the art in the drop.

Why can't you ship to my city?

Matches are dangerous goods: UN 1944, Class 4.1 flammable solids. Shiprocket, Blue Dart and India Post all refuse them, and we are not going to lie on a shipping form. So Drop 01 is hand-delivered by us inside Pune only. Join the waitlist and you get first access when we can reach your city.

Is the counter real?

Yes, and it is deliberately boring. The "0 of 500 claimed" number is typed in by us after we count real orders. Nothing on this page makes it tick up on its own to rush you.

Do the matches actually work?

Yes. They are real safety matches made by a licensed match manufacturer. Strike them on the box side like any match.

Are they safe?

They are matches, so treat them like matches. Keep away from children, keep the box dry, strike away from your face. Full safety text is printed on every box.

What if it sells out before I pay?

Then the page flips to sold out and stops taking orders, and the waitlist stays open. Waitlist order decides who gets offered Drop 02 first.

Can I buy more than one?

Up to 10 boxes per person while stock lasts, so more people get one.

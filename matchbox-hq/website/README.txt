THIS FOLDER IS YOUR WEBSITE.

Double-click index.html to see it right now, offline, on this computer.

THE SITE HAS TWO SIGN-UP FORMS:
  THE WAITLIST  - always on, never closes. Name + WhatsApp number.
                  This is your standing audience for every drop.
  THE PRE-ORDER - only appears while the drop window is open. This is the
                  one with the "X of 500 claimed" counter, and the one that
                  can take money.
When the drop is closed, sold out, or has not opened yet, the pre-order form
hides itself and the page points people at the waitlist instead.

>>> BEFORE YOU POST THE LINK ON INSTAGRAM <<<
Do PART 5 of the guide. Until you do, signups are only saved on the phone
of the person who typed them. That is a test safety net, NOT a way to
collect other people's details.

payment mode switch, your Instagram, and where signups go.
